# Returns | COACH

Shop for gifts without worry, thanks to extended holiday returns. All purchases made between Wednesday, October 28, 2026 through Friday, December 25, 2026 can be returned through Monday, January 25, 2027.

We want you to love your Coach product, but we get that sometimes it doesn't work out. If you aren't satisfied with your purchase, please return it to us by mail or to a  [Coach or Coach Outlet store](https://www.coach.com/stores/store-locator) within 30 days for a refund in your original form of payment. Items must be unworn, unused and include all tags, parts and accessories. Note that shipping and gift box costs are non-refundable.

[START YOUR RETURN](https://returns.narvar.com/coachorders/returns?locale=en_US "Start your return")

| TYPE OF ITEM | RETURN TIMEFRAME |
| --- | --- |
| Regular Price | 30 Days |
| Sale Items (excluding final sale) | 30 Days |
| Customized Items (Coach Create or monogrammed items and cut-to-size belts) | Final Sale |
| Made To Order | Final Sale |
| Leather Care Products | Final Sale |
| Fragrances | Final Sale |

Made-to-order, customized or altered items cannot be returned or exchanged. This includes pieces personalized via Coach Create, bags with monogrammed hangtags and belts cut to size. However, if you're not satisfied with your customized Coach Create piece, bring it to a Coach store with customization services—our craftsmen will switch out your embellishments for free.

##   
Return to Store:

Whether your items were purchased in-store or online from Coach or Coach Outlet, you're welcome to return them at Coach and Coach Outlet stores—the choice is yours. We offer full refunds (except for shipping and gift box costs) when you return items in-person. Find your nearest store [here](https://www.coach.com/stores/store-locator).

##   
Return by Mail:

You can also return your new and unused item(s) by mail using a [pre-paid shipping label](https://returns.narvar.com/coachorders/returns?locale=en_US) or by shipping via insured, traceable means (such as UPS, USPS, and FedEx) to the following address:

CSI Returns  
1549 Vantage Way  
Jacksonville, FL 32218

When using our pre-paid label, $7 will be deducted from your refund.

Once your return is received, it could take up to 7–10 business days for it to be processed. Please allow 1–2 billing cycles for your financial institution to credit your account.

Merchandise returned with a gift receipt will receive a Coach Merchandise Card (store credit).
