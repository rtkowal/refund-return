# Return Policy | Office Depot

January 12, 2022

  

Thank you for shopping with us. If for some reason, you’re not 100% satisfied with your purchase, you can

return or exchange quickly and easily. Here’s everything you need to know!

## **Return in store - Fast & simple**  

If you purchased in store or online, just bring your item(s) with your Original Receipt to a checkout counter in any of our 1,000+ stores for your refund or exchange within the required time frame. No receipt? No problem! Many items can still be returned or exchanged in store with a valid ID. For more details on our return policy, see below.

[Find a store](https://www.officedepot.com/storelocator/?promo_name=marketing&promo_id=marketing-returns-in-store&promo_creative=returns_in-store&promo_position=help_returns_main_4_1_0)

  

## **Return by mail**  

If you made your purchase online, go to your order history page, select the order you want to return then select "Return" in the upper right corner. Select the item(s) you want to return or exchange and print a FREE return label and drop your item off at any carrier location. You will need an online account in order to process your return or exchange by mail. For more details on our return policy, see below.

[Go to your order history to begin](https://www.officedepot.com/orderhistory/orderHistoryList.do)

  

## **Classroom Wish List Returns**  

If you received items from a Classroom Wish List and need to return them, find your delivery confirmation email and present that email to the cashier at your local Office Depot or OfficeMax store to get a Return Card. The amount on the Return Card will be the amount that was paid for the item by the buyer. For more details on our return policy, see below.

[Find a store](https://www.officedepot.com/storelocator/?promo_name=marketing&promo_id=marketing-returns-in-store&promo_creative=returns_in-store&promo_position=help_returns_main_4_1_0)

**Return Policy - Frequently Asked Questions**

**How to package my return?**  
Please make sure that you package, label, and ship the correct item, including all return documentation for a refund. These items will be held for 10 days and then donated/recycled. 

Office Depot and Veyer, our affiliate company for supply chain services, will not compensate for wrong or mistaken returns. If you think that you may have sent the wrong item by accident, contact Customer Service at 1-800-GO-DEPOT (1-800-463-3768). Veyer cannot guarantee that the item will be found. If the item is found, Veyer will not be responsible to ship the item back to the customer. The customer must provide Veyer with a UPS or FedEx account number or label or other shipping method to return the item.

### **How and when will I be refunded?**  

Items returned in our stores will have faster refund processing times.

Items returned with an original register receipt, eReceipt, packing slip, or order confirmation will follow these guidelines ("Original Receipt"):

*   If you paid with cash or a check written more than 10 days ago, you will be refunded in cash.  
    
*   If you paid with a check written less than 10 days ago or with an Office Depot OfficeMax Gift Card, you will be refunded with an Office Depot OfficeMax Return Card which you will receive within 3-4 weeks.  
    
*   If you paid with a debit card, you will be refunded based on your bank's processing time.  
    
*   If you paid with a credit card, you will be refunded based on your card company's processing time.  
    
*   If you paid with an Office Depot OfficeMax rewards certificate, you will be refunded by check within 3-4 weeks. 
*   If you paid by PayPal, please see the PayPal refund details. [https://www.paypal.com/vg/smarthelp/article/i-want-a-refund.-how-do-refunds-work-faq3248](https://www.paypal.com/vg/smarthelp/article/i-want-a-refund.-how-do-refunds-work-faq3248)

### **What items can be returned/exchanged without a receipt?**

All returns without an Original Receipt require valid government identification. Technology products without Original Receipt are not eligible for return.

**Software, CDs, DVDs, and Video Games**  
Without an Original Receipt, these items are eligible for an exact-item exchange only.

**All other**  
All other returns without an Original Receipt for items that are active in our computer system will be refunded in the form of an Office Depot® or OfficeMax® Return Card in an amount equal to the lowest retail price during the preceding 90 days. Office Depot and OfficeMax reserve the right to request identification or deny any exchange or return.

### **What if I placed my order online and picked it up in store?**

You may return or exchange your item in store. Please follow the instructions above.

### **What if my item(s) was from a manufacturer direct supplier?**

Catalog and Website purchases may be returned/exchanged in accordance with our return policy in any store or by going to your order history and selecting the item to return or exchange by mail.
