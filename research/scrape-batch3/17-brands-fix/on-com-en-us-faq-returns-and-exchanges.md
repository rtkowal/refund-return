# Returns and exchanges | On United States

## Returns

Any product purchased between October 21 and December 31, 2026 in the following regions will benefit from extended 90 day returns for a refund or exchange: Australia, Brazil, Canada, Europe, Hong Kong, Malaysia, New Zealand, the Philippines, Singapore, South Korea, Taiwan, Thailand, the United Kingdom and the United States. For all other countries, our standard 30 day return and exchange policy applies.

Returns must be in new condition, and returned in their original, intact packaging (including apparel and accessories). We are only able to accept returns for orders purchased directly from On.

To submit a return request, enter your order number and the email address used to place the order [here](https://www.on.com/returns).

We cover the cost of shipping for eligible returned On products unless it is to, or from, one of the following countries: India, Indonesia, Thailand and Malaysia.

Please note that expedited shipping costs on the original outbound order will not be refunded in the case of returns or exchanges.

Limited editions and last season items can only be refunded, but are not exchangeable due to limited stock.

## Refunds

Your refund will be issued after our warehouse team has received your return and checked in and verified the items. This process can take up to 14 business days. You will receive a confirmation email when the refund has been processed, and it will then take an additional 2-5 business days for the funds to be reflected back to your original payment method. We appreciate your patience while we work to process your return.

## Track my return

After you have submitted a return request and shipped the item(s) back to us, you will be able to check in on the progress of your return. You can do this either by using the link in your return confirmation email, by checking the ‘order history’ section of your On account, or by entering your order number and the email address used to place the order [here](https://www.on.com/order-tracker).
