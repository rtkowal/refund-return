# Returns & Exchanges | Nordstrom

Use one of our convenient options to return any item purchased at a Nordstrom store or online.

## Make a Free Return in Store or by Mail

#### Bring to a Store

*   Go to any register for assistance.
*   Refunds can be expected in 3-5 business days.

#### Send by Mail

*   Start your return online.
*   We'll email you a prepaid shipping label to print at home and attach to your package.
*   No printer? Show your QR code at an eligible USPS or FedEx location and they'll print a shipping label for you.
*   Returns are processed in 8-12 business days and refunds can be expected 3-5 business days after processing. For Marketplace items sold by third-party sellers, expect refunds within 10-14 business days of shipping the return, including transit and processing time.

## Our Return Policy

We handle returns on a case-by-case basis with the ultimate goal of making our customers happy. We stand behind our goods and services, and want customers to be satisfied with them. We'll always do our best to take care of customers—our philosophy is to deal with them fairly and reasonably. We have long believed that when we treat our customers fairly, they in turn are fair with us. We apply refunds to the tender with which returned items were purchased. If we choose to provide a refund and no record of sale is available, we'll ask for personal identification and a refund will be provided at the current price on a Nordstrom gift card.

**Need to make a return?** We no longer include return labels with orders, but you can still print your own prepaid label, get a QR code to use at eligible USPS or FedEx locations or make your return in store. Please be sure to pair the item with the correct package and label. Items sent to us by mistake, with the wrong label, or that do not meet our return criteria won't be returned to you or refunded.

**We also accept eligible returns of Nordstrom and Nordstrom.com merchandise at Nordstrom Rack locations.** Fine jewelry and designer items purchased at Nordstrom or Nordstrom.com are not eligible to be returned to Nordstrom Rack stores. Boutique returns must be processed at the store where they were originally purchased.

#### Additional Policies

**Gift Cards:** Gift cards can't be returned.

**Balsam Hill:** Returns/exchanges are accepted within 30 days of purchase or prior to December 25 (whichever comes first). Please call Nordstrom customer care at 1.888.282.6060 to request a return shipping label. Nordstrom will not accept Balsam Hill products in stores or by mail.

**Car Seats & Bases:** Car seats and bases can only be returned in their original, unopened boxes.

**Casper Bed:** Please call 1.888.282.6060 for inquiries and assistance.

**Fine Jewelry:** For your protection, fine jewelry cannot be returned by mail. Please return fine jewelry to any Nordstrom store that has a Fine Jewelry department. Bring your order confirmation (email or digital receipt) and/or the credit card used when you placed the order.

**WATCHFINDER & CO.:** To guarantee merchandise authenticity and to preserve its condition and value, all Watchfinder & Co. pre-owned items purchased from Nordstrom (in store and online) are ineligible for refund or exchange. This Final Sale policy supersedes Nordstrom and [Nordstrom.com](http://nordstrom.com/) return policies.

**Nordstrom Rack Return Policy:** Please visit the [Nordstrom Rack](https://www.nordstromrack.com/) site for information about their return policy.

**Marketplace & Third Party Sellers:** Return policies differ for Marketplace purchases from third-party sellers. Please refer to the item page or your order history for the specific return window and return policy details. Please note that Marketplace products are not eligible for exchanges, price adjustments, or expedited shipping services.

Questions? Call 1.888.282.6060.
