# Can I return Panties?

**Any item(s) purchased that you don't love may be returned,** including Sale & Clearance and items received as gifts.

EXCLUSIONS: Items where the sewn-in garment tag has been cut or removed. Price tags are not always included with an online purchase and are not required. Original shipping and handling fees are not refundable.
