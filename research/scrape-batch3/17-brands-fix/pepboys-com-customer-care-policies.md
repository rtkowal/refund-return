# Customer Care: Policies | Pep Boys

## **Cancelation and Refund Policy**

If you wish to cancel your order, please contact our support team at [1-800-PEP-BOYS](tel:18007372697) (737-2697) or [contactus@pepboys.com](mailto:contactus@pepboys.com). You will need to provide the following information:

*   Customer Name
*   Customer Phone Number
*   Work Order #
*   Web Order #
*   _Deposit $ Amount (when available)_

We will process your refund and credit the payment card you used to place the order in the amount of your order, less the 20% restocking fee. Please note that it may take up to ten business days for the credit to appear on your account, due to credit card company regulations.

## **Return and Exchange Policy**

Our goal is to ensure our customers are completely satisfied - we deliver on promises and honor commitments. If for any reason you are not satisfied, subject to the applicable warranty and the limitations and exclusions below, simply return with the original receipt within 30 days of the purchase date for a refund or replacement.

#### **Service Warranty**

Automotive service and installed parts are warranted for 90 days or 3,000 miles, whichever comes first, unless different warranty terms are specified on your Invoice. Please see the “Limited Warranties” page of your Invoice for further details regarding the warranty on service and installed parts.

#### **Extended Labor Warranty (ELW) Cancellation**

Pep Boys offers an optional Extended Labor Warranty to customers for purchase with select services, which extends the limited warranty to 12 months with unlimited mileage. ELW can be canceled within 30 days of purchase. Simply fill out a cancellation form and send it to the Warranty Administrator. The Pep Boys store where ELW was purchased will refund the cost with confirmation of cancellation (as provided by Warranty Administrator to customer).

#### **Ride Happiness Guarantee**

Pep Boys offers a 30-days or 1,000-mile satisfaction guarantee on Pep Boys installed tires. If you are not 100% satisfied with your new tires, we will exchange them for tires better suited for your needs.

*   The original purchase price will be credited to the sale of the new tires.
*   Ride Happiness Guarantee requires purchase of 4 tires
*   Customer is responsible for payment of installation of new tires (mounting, balancing, valve stem or tpms kit, applicable taxes / fees). If road hazard was purchased initially, it can be credited and applied to new tires. Road hazard warranty for new tires may have a price difference.
*   Tire(s) must have only been used on the vehicle on which it was originally installed.
*   Tires damaged due to road hazards, accidents, negligence, or abuse are excluded.
*   Ride Happiness Guarantee is limited to one set of tires per vehicle within any 12-month period.

#### **No Receipt?**

No receipt, no problem. If you do not have your receipt, you may return your purchase for an in-store credit at our lowest selling price during the previous 30 days. You will need to provide a valid form of identification to return an item without a receipt.

#### **Paid by Check?**

If you paid by check we unfortunately cannot accept your return until the 15th day after the date of purchase.

#### **Sales Tax Refund**

In some states, sales tax is non-refundable without a receipt, or after a state-specified time period. If your purchase was made in one of these states the refund of any sales tax you paid will be in accordance with applicable state law.

#### **Products with Return Limitations**

*   Batteries / Alternators / Starters – eligible for exchange if verified defective via Pep Boys’ in-store testing within warranty period
*   Special Order merchandise, uninstalled tires and / or uninstalled tire chains will be accepted for return subject to a 20% restocking fee

#### **Exclusions**

*   If installed, performance accessories, electrical items, electronics, and tire chains cannot be accepted for return
*   Opened or consumed products cannot be accepted for return
*   Clearance merchandise is not eligible for return
*   The eligibility for return of Outside purchased parts (for example, items Pep Boys is required to procure to satisfy a customer’s special request or services that are outsourced to a third party) may be subject to the restrictions of the supplier.
*   Parts / products purchased from Auto Plus cannot be exchanged by or returned to Pep Boys
