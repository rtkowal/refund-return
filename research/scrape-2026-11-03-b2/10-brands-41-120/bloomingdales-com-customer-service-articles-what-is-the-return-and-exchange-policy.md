# Bloomingdale's Return Policy

We hope you love your Bloomingdale's purchase. If you change your mind, most saleable merchandise can be returned within **30 days of receipt** with proof of purchase for a refund to your original form of payment.

#### Return Policy Exceptions

The following items have different return requirements:

*   **CHANEL, myGemma, REBAG, and other pre-owned merchandise** may be returned within **14 days of purchase**.
*   **Final Sale items** cannot be returned or exchanged, including:
    *   Items marked **"The Final Offer"**
    *   Altered, customized, monogrammed, or personalized products
    *   Breast pumps
    *   Food and beverage items
    *   Sexual wellness products

*   For safety reasons, the following items must be **unused, unopened, and sealed in their original packaging**:
    *   **Baby & Child Products**
        *   Baby strollers
        *   Car seats
        *   Highchairs
        *   Playpens
        *   Bouncers
        *   Changing tables
        *   Dressers
        *   Cribs
        *   Bassinets
    *   **Other Products**
        *   Food processors
        *   LYMA products

*   **Furniture, mattresses, area rugs, and items sold and shipped by a third-party seller** have special return policies.

#### Return Requirements

To be eligible for a return, merchandise must:

*   Be in the same condition as when received (unused, unworn, and undamaged)
*   Include all original tags
*   Include original packaging

#### Gifts With Purchase

If your order included a gift with purchase, the gift must be returned along with the qualifying item(s). If it is not returned, the value of the gift may be deducted from your refund, or your return may not be accepted.

#### Outlet Store Returns

Merchandise purchased at **bloomingdales.com** or at a **Bloomingdale's store** cannot be returned or exchanged at a **Bloomingdale's Outlet Store**.

#### Additional Information

Bloomingdale's reserves the right to determine whether returned merchandise is in saleable condition. While we may not be able to accommodate every return, we value our customers and will always strive to be fair and reasonable when reviewing return requests.

For complete details, including special return policies for furniture, mattresses, rugs, and third-party seller items, see our **[Return Policy FAQs](https://www.bloomingdales.com/customer-service/category/returns)**.
