#### RETURNING CARHARTT.COM ORDERS

[START YOUR USA RETURN](https://returns.narvar.com/carhartt/returns?locale=en_US)[START YOUR CANADA RETURN](https://returns.narvar.com/carhartt/returns?locale=en_CA)

Thank you for your order! Carhartt has been outworking them all since 1889, and we pride ourselves on the quality of our hardworking clothing. We hope you’re happy with your order. If you are not satisfied for any reason, we will be happy to accept a return. To begin your return, find the order number that appears on the order or shipping confirmation emails.

We only accept returns from orders placed on Carhartt.com. All refunds will be credited in the method of payment you used on the original purchase. Original shipping costs, if any, are unfortunately non-refundable.

Please contact us if a product is defective, damaged, or different from what you ordered at 1-800-833-3118.

**How to make a return:**

**STEP 1:** Begin by entering your order number and email address.

**STEP 2:** Select the items and reason for the return.

**STEP 3:** Include the Carhartt Return Copy in your return package so we can process the return.

**STEP 4:** Attach the UPS Return Address Label found in the return portal to your package.

If you do not have the original packing slip, please include a note inside the return package with the following information: reason for returning item(s), order number, purchaser’s name, address, and telephone number. Your order number can be found in your confirmation/shipment email, through Order History in ‘My Account’ on Carhartt.com, or by calling Carhartt Customer Service at 1-800-833-3118. To find the UPS location nearest to you, please go to: www.ups.com/dropoff. Still have questions about Carhartt.com returns? Email [Just\_Ask\_Us@Carhartt.com](mailto:Just_Ask_Us@carhartt.com) or give us a call at 1-800-833-3118.

How long do I have to return my purchase?

You may return your purchase within 180 days of your order, in original condition with the tags attached.

Can I exchange my purchase?

We're sorry but we are unable to perform exchanges at this time.

Retail In-Store Return Policy

Carhartt stores will accept returns for items purchased from Carhartt within 180 days of the original purchase. Returned items should be in the original purchased condition, with tags attached, and the original purchase receipt.

**WITH RECEIPT** Refunds and exchanges made with a receipt will be processed at the original purchase price, plus applicable sales tax, if returned within 180 days of the purchase. Such items may be exchanged for the same product or refunded in the original payment method. Returns accompanied by a gift receipt can be exchanged for another item or for a gift card equal to the price of the item at the time of the purchase. If returned after 180 days from purchase, the amount paid by the consumer will be processed as a merchandise credit.

**WITHOUT RECEIPT** Refunds or exchanges made without a receipt will be processed at the current sale price in store, plus applicable sales tax as long as the item is found within our POS system. Such items may be exchanged for the same product or returned for merchandise credit. Items not found in the store POS cannot be exchanged or returned in store. Carhartt reserves the right to limit the number of returns made without a store receipt

**PRICE MATCH** Carhartt resellers are free to sell Carhartt items at a price of their choosing. Carhartt Retail stores and Carhartt.com will not offer price matching to consumers if an item is found for a lower price elsewhere.

**WARRANTY** These policies can be accessed on the [Warranty and Commitment](https://www.carhartt.com/our-commitment) page. Carhartt, Inc. assumes no liability for garments with alterations, embroidery, or other ornamentation added to the garment. Garments with alteration made in store by a Carhartt associate may not be returned or exchanged after the consumer signs off on the completed work.

Since all apparel has an expected service life, this commitment does not cover damage caused by normal wear and tear (including zippers and other hardware), misuse, neglect, or obvious abuse of the garment.

**ECOMMERCE PURCHASES RETURNED/EXCHANGED IN-STORE** Consumers who make purchases on Carhartt.com may return items within 180 days of purchase by requesting their refund through the Carhartt return portal on Carhartt.com. Consumers may initiate this process, or the process can be completed in-store with the assistance of a store associate.

Factory In-Store Return Policy

Carhartt factory stores will accept returns for items that were purchased at discounted pricing in Factory stores. Returned items should be in the original purchased condition, unwashed and unworn with the original purchase receipt.

Factory store will not accept returns on items purchased on clearance as these sales are final. Factory stores will not accept returns for Carhartt gear purchased elsewhere including other retail store or online. Consumers attempting to make returns from other locations should be directed to Carhartt Customer Service 1-800-833-3118, or the original location of purchase.

**With Receipt**

*   Refunds and exchanges made with a receipt will be processed at the original purchase price, plus applicable sales tax, if returned within 60 days of purchase. If the consumer does not have the original form of payment the refund will be processed as a merchandise credit.
*   Such items may be exchanged for the same product or refunded in the original payment method.
*   Returns accompanied by a gift receipt can be exchanged for a gift card equal to the price of the item at the time of the purchase.

**Without Receipt**

*   Only items sold within the Factory store may be returned without a receipt. Carhartt reserves the right to refuse a return if the item was not offered in the Factory stores assortment. Refunds or exchanges made without a receipt will be processed at the lowest sale price, plus applicable sales tax. Such items may be exchanged for the same product or returned for merchandise credit. Carhartt reserves the right to limit the number of returns made without a store receipt.

**Other** Carhartt, Inc. assumes no liability for garments that have had alterations, embroidery, or other ornamentation added to the garment outside of a Carhartt store. Items that have been altered by a Carhartt store must be returned or exchanged to the store that performed the alterations.

Since all apparel has an expected service life, this commitment does not cover damage caused by normal wear and tear (including zippers and other hardware), misuse, neglect, or obvious abuse of the garment.

When will I get my refund?

After we receive your return, please allow 5-7 business days for the credit to be released back to the original method of payment.
