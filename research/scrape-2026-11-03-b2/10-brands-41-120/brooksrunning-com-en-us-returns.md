## Returns center

**Take your Brooks gear for a 90-day trial run and if you aren't satisfied, return them for free** – seriously we mean it; no shipping or re‐stocking fee. For more information check out our [FAQs](https://support.brooksrunning.com/hc/en-us/sections/360002204451-Returns-and-Exchanges "Returns and exchanges").

If you believe your product is defective, please complete our [Product Support form](https://support.brooksrunning.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=360000168152 "Product Support form") .

If you would like to exchange product that you received as a gift, please [contact us](https://support.brooksrunning.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=360000171931 "Product Support form").
