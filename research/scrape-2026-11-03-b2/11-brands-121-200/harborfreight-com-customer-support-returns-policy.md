# Return Policy | Customer Support

If you are unsatisfied with an item you purchased from Harbor Freight, you may return it within 90 days of the purchase date for a refund or exchange.

Once we process your refund, it may take up to 10 business days for the funds to be credited to your account. In most cases, refunds will automatically go back to the original form of payment used for the purchase. Purchases made with a Harbor Freight gift card will be refunded to a new Harbor Freight gift card.

Proof of purchase is required for all returns. Visit our [Terms and Conditions page](https://www.harborfreight.com/terms-and-conditions) for our full Return Policy.

A restocking fee may apply to an item being returned. See [Restocking Fees](https://www.harborfreight.com/customer-support/returns/restocking) for more information.

For in-store returns, a valid government-issued photo ID and/or other identifying information must be presented at the time of return. We maintain a database of consumer activity and other information collected in connection with a purchase or a return, which we use to authorize returns and may share with third parties that provide fraud prevention services.

Need to return an item? Visit the [Returning an Item](https://www.harborfreight.com/customer-support/returns/returning-an-item) page for instructions on returning in-store and online purchases.

### Exceptions

Pumps, sprayers, and drain cleaners cannot be returned to stores or by mail if opened due to safety reasons. See [Returning Pumps, Sprayers, and Drain Cleaners](https://www.harborfreight.com/customer-support/returns/pumps-sprayers) for complete details.

Items cannot contain engine oil or fuel in them at the time of the return. Please drain all the fuel and engine oil before returning your gas-powered item to any Harbor Freight store. Due to safety hazards, items that use flammable liquids or gases or contain hazardous materials cannot be returned through the mail even if the items were drained of the materials.

Open Box and As-Is Items can be returned within five (5) days of purchase to any Harbor Freight store. See [Open Box and As-Is Items](https://www.harborfreight.com/customer-support/store-info/open-box) for more information.

Select items, including abrasive media, can only be returned in-store if unopened, including items purchased online. Visit the product page for your specific item for more information.

### Nonreturnable/Nonrefundable Items

Replacement parts are final sale and cannot be returned. Gift card purchases are nonrefundable.
