# Returns & Exchanges

**What is Journeys’ refund and exchange policy?**

We accept returns or exchanges on merchandise in new condition, with original packaging and valid proof of purchase (such as a receipt or online order confirmation), within 60 days of purchase. Shipping charges are non-refundable.

For orders placed before April 1, 2026, the return policy in effect at the time of purchase will apply. These orders may be returned or exchanged within 365 days of the original purchase date, as long as all other return policy requirements are met. Orders placed on or after April 1, 2026 are eligible for return or exchange within 60 days of the original purchase date.

**How do I make a return to store?**

Visit any Journeys store for FREE returns or exchanges. Just bring the item in original packaging and proof of purchase.

**How do I make a return online?**

**For purchases made online:**

Start your return or exchange [here](https://journeys.reverselogix.io/portal/res/return/t3/JYN/cc/index/en-us). A $7.99 fee will be deducted for using the prepaid label. Please allow 2–4 weeks for processing.

 For orders placed before April 1, 2026, please contact Customer Service if you need a return shipping label.

**For purchases made in-store:**

You can return or exchange your item for free at any Journeys store. Prefer to send it back? Just fill out the [Return/Exchange Form](https://www.journeys.com/help?a=Return-Exchange-Form---id--ME1N75tsSoGMJpvnf3c3FA) and ship your item(s) to the address below using the carrier of your choice.

Need a return shipping label? Contact [Customer Service](https://www.journeys.com/help) and we’ll be happy to help create one for you.
