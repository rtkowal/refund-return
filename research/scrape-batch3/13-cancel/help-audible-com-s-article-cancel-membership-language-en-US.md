# Cancel membership

You can cancel your membership by visiting [Membership details](https://www.audible.com/account/membership/cancel/benefits-reminder) on the Audible site. Note: Deleting the Audible app will not cancel a membership.

You can [cancel your membership](https://www.audible.com/account/membership/cancel/show-offers) on the Audible website anytime. You’ll still have access to any titles you bought with [credits](https://help.audible.com/s/article/learn-about-credits?language=en_US) or with a credit/debit card. If you have credits but don't want to lose them when you cancel, you may be eligible to [pause your membership](https://help.audible.com/s/article/pause-your-membership).

**Note**: There are different steps for canceling if you are billed through the [App Store](https://help.audible.com/s/article/manage-app-store-subscription?language=en_US) or [Google Play Store](https://help.audible.com/s/article/manage-google-play-store-subscription?language=en_US). For specific instructions, view [Manage App Store subscription](https://help.audible.com/s/article/manage-app-store-subscription?language=en_US) and [Manage Google Play Store subscription](https://help.audible.com/s/article/manage-google-play-store-subscription?language=en_US).

## How do I cancel my membership on the Audible site?

**On desktop**

1.  Select your name on the top navigation.
2.  Select **Membership details**.
3.  Select the [Cancel membership](https://www.audible.com/account/membership/cancel/show-offers) link.
4.  Select **Confirm cancellation**.

**Note**: After you cancel, you'll get an email confirmation. Your membership status on your Membership details page will also reflect this change.

**On mobile web**

1.  Select the main menu.
2.  Select your name.
3.  Select **Membership details**.
4.  Select **Cancel membership**.
5.  Select **Confirm cancellation**.

**Note**: After you cancel, you'll get an email confirmation. Your membership status on your Membership details page will also reflect this change.

**Note**: If you don't see the cancel option, verify that you're signed into the account with the active membership. If you still don’t see the option to cancel, you might have multiple accounts. Please try signing in with a different email address.

## How do I cancel my Audible membership billed through Apple or Google?

Audible memberships billed through Apple or Google cannot be canceled through the Audible site and must be canceled through the App Store or Google Play Store. For specific instructions to cancel, view the following articles:

*   [Manage App Store Subscription](https://help.audible.com/s/article/manage-app-store-subscription)
*   [Manage Google Play Store Subscription](https://help.audible.com/s/article/manage-google-play-store-subscription)

## What happens if I cancel?

At the end of your final [billing cycle](https://www.audible.com/account/overview), Audible Premium members will lose access to all unused credits and [member benefits](https://help.audible.com/s/article/learn-about-premium-plus?language=en_US), including exclusive discounts and [included titles](https://help.audible.com/s/article/learn-about-the-plus-catalog?language=en_US).

Note:

*   [Credits](https://help.audible.com/s/article/learn-about-credits?language=en_US) expire 12 months after their issue date, or upon cancellation.
*   [Credits](https://help.audible.com/s/article/learn-about-credits?language=en_US) received through an App Store membership or a Google Play Store membership do not expire and will remain in your account after cancellation.

## Can I still access my Library when I cancel?

You can listen to titles you purchased with a [credit](https://help.audible.com/s/article/learn-about-credits?language=en_US) or a credit/debit card anytime. They're yours to keep and will always be available in your [Library](https://www.audible.com/library/titles) whether you're a current member or not. You can [download titles](https://help.audible.com/s/article/download-titles?language=en_US) you've purchased as many times as you want, even if you cancel your membership.

However, you'll lose access to any [included titles](https://help.audible.com/s/article/learn-about-the-plus-catalog?language=en_US) you've downloaded the included catalog, and these titles [will be locked](https://help.audible.com/s/article/title-in-library-is-locked?language=en_US).

Notes:

*   You can continue to use your account to purchase titles at full price.
*   You can purchase locked titles if you'd like to own them or become a member again to regain access.
*   If no purchase options are available, we no longer carry the title.

## Do I keep my credits when I cancel?

No. Your unused [credits](https://help.audible.com/s/article/learn-about-credits?language=en_US) will be lost along with your other [membership benefits](https://help.audible.com/s/article/learn-about-premium-plus?language=en_US). Any title you get with a credit is yours to keep, though, so use your credits before the end of your final [billing period](https://www.audible.com/account/overview).

**Note**:

*   Credits expire 12 months after their issue date, or upon cancellation.
*   Credits received through an App Store membership or a Google Play Store membership do not expire and will remain in your account after cancellation.

## Can I purchase titles after I cancel?

Yes, you can [purchase titles](https://help.audible.com/s/article/buy-an-audiobook?language=en_US) with or without an Audible membership. Any title you purchase is yours to keep, regardless of your membership status.
