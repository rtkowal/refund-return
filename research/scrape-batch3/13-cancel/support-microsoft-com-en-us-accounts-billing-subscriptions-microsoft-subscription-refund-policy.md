# Microsoft subscription refund policy | Microsoft Support

Subscriptions purchased directly from Microsoft may be eligible for a refund, depending on the timing of cancellation, the subscription type, and the country of purchase.  

Note

*   If you purchased a Microsoft subscription from Google Play or the Apple app store, contact their customer support for cancellation or refund. For Play Store purchases, go to [Learn about Google Play refund policies](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637). For Apple App Store purchases, request a refund from [Apple support](https://support.apple.com/118428).
*   If you purchased your subscription in Germany, you can cancel without signing into your Microsoft account. You'll need a few pieces of information to do so. [Cancel a Microsoft subscription acquired in Germany](https://aka.ms/germancancel).
*   For refund information on Xbox subscriptions, please see [Xbox subscription cancellations and refunds](https://support.xbox.com/help/subscriptions-billing/manage-subscriptions/cancel-recurring-billing-or-subscription).

## Microsoft subscription refund window

In most cases, you may receive a refund within 30 days of your initial purchase or before your first recurring billing date (whichever is sooner) by canceling your subscription in the steps that are outlined in the section [How to request a refund](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accounts-billing/subscriptions/microsoft-subscription-refund-policy#how-to-request-a-refund).

In addition, you’re entitled to a refund of your most recent recurring billing charge if you cancel your subscription within 30 days after payment. This refund right is limited to one time per Microsoft account, per subscription product.

This refund policy doesn’t affect any additional rights you may have under applicable law and doesn’t apply to prepaid retail subscription codes, which are subject to the return policies of the retailer from which you purchased the code.

In some countries/regions, Microsoft may offer a prorated refund when an eligible subscription is cancelled after billing, in line with local consumer protection rules. Availability varies by region and subscription terms - see below for details:

## How to request a refund

Important

*   Not all cancellations result in a refund.
*   Eligibility is determined automatically during the cancellation process.
*   Sign in with the Microsoft account that was used to make the purchase.
*   Access to Microsoft 365 typically continues until the end of the current billing period if no refund is issued.

Sign in to your Microsoft account, navigate to **Services & subscriptions**, select the subscription, and choose **Manage** followed by **Cancel**.

During cancellation, we will indicate whether the subscription qualifies for a refund and guide the user through the next steps.
