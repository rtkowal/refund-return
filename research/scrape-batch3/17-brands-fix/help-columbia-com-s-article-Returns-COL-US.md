# Returns

## **Returns** 

### We get it, sometimes things don't work out. We just require that returned products be in original, unworn condition with the tags on, within 60 days of purchasing. 

*   **Apparel:** Must be unworn, with tags attached.
    *   If a clothing hang tag has been accidentally removed but the item is still in new condition, you may still attempt a return. Please note that acceptance of such returns is subject to inspection by our warehouse team, who will determine eligibility based on the item’s condition. 
*   **Footwear:** Must include the original box in new condition. 

Have an online purchase you'd like to return or exchange? **[Get started here.](https://www.columbia.com/order-returns/?r=1)**

### Here's for the nitty gritty details:

*   Items may be returned or exchanged within 60 days of the original purchase date.
*    Refunds are issued to the original payment method when a receipt is provided or the purchase is found in your customer account.
    *   Purchases made with merchandise credit or gift cards are refunded as merchandise credit.

### **In-Store Purchases**

*   Purchases made in-store must be returned in-store.
*   In-store purchases returned without a receipt require a government-issued photo ID and will be refunded at the lowest price sold in the past 60 days.
*   Find your nearest store location here: [Store Locator](https://www.columbia.com/stores?r=1 "Store Locator") (filter by Brand and Factory Store then enter your zip code)

**Online Purchases**

*   Online purchases can be dropped at Columbia Branded or Factory store locations for return. Just bring your order confirmation email. 
    *   Returns sent in with no proof of online purchase will be returned to sender.
*   A **$6 return shipping fee** is deducted from your refund to cover the return shipping cost. 
    *   Items must be received at our warehouse within 60 days of purchase.
    *   Refunds are issue 7-10 business days after arriving at the warehouse.
    *   For locations not serviced by our UPS shipping labels (e.g., Guam, Puerto Rico, Alaska), a USPS label will be provided.
    *   Other unsupported locations may require you to use your own shipping method. Please record your tracking number for confirmation as we are not responsible for your item while in transit. Mail returns to:
        *   Columbia Sportswear Returns  
            6940 N Leadbetter Ave  
            Portland, OR 97203  
            

* * *

### **Returns with Loyalty Rewards**

*   **In-store returns**: Rewards points will be refunded back to the purchaser's account.
*   **Online orders**:   
    *   Returns made by mail: CGR points will be refunded back to the purchaser's CGR account.
    *   Returns made in-store: CGR points will be refunded in the form of a physical gift card.
