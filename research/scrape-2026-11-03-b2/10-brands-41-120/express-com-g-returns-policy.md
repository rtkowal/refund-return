# Returns Policy

## Returns & Exchanges

Online & In-Store Policy

Don't love what you bought? We're happy to accept returns for items with a valid receipt or order number within 30 days of the delivery date.

**During the Holiday season, we are temporarily changing our return policy as follows:**

With your original receipt or order confirmation, merchandise purchased between Sunday, November 15th, 2026 and Thursday, December 31st, 2026 may be returned for a full refund of the purchase price to your original form of payment.

With a gift receipt, merchandise purchased between Sunday, November 15th, 2026 and Thursday, December 31st, 2026 may be returned for a store credit of the purchase price.

If you do not have a receipt, we will conduct a courtesy search of our database to locate your transaction. If we are unable to find your transaction, you may exchange up to three pieces of merchandise for merchandise equal or greater in value (based on the current selling price of the merchandise). You would be responsible for paying the difference if the exchanged merchandise exceeds the value of the merchandise being returned.

All other terms and conditions of our Return Policy will apply. Please see our full return policy in stores or at express.com for more details. This Holiday Return Policy Extension ends on January 31, 2027.

We're happy to accept returns for items within 30 days of the delivery date with a valid receipt. We do not accept returns or exchanges without a receipt, invoice, or order confirmation. If you do not have a valid receipt or if we cannot confirm your purchase in our system, no refund, credit or courtesy exchange will be issued.  

*   Merchandise must be returned in its original condition, unwashed and unworn (this does not include merchandise that is defective).
*   Certain merchandise such as fragrance or swimwear may not be eligible for return unless in original condition.
*   Shipping and handling fees are not refundable.
*   To ensure a fair experience for everyone, we reserve the right to limit or decline returns and exchanges if we detect patterns of policy abuse.

###### Clearance

Items marked Clearance or items that have a price ending in .97 are not returnable. Face coverings are also non-returnable.

###### Express Items

Online purchases shipped to a U.S. address can be returned by mail (see below) or to any Express retail store in the U.S. within 30 days of the delivery date.

Returns will not be accepted in stores outside the U.S. or at Express Factory Outlet stores. See “Starting a Return” or “Returning to Stores” sections below for assistance.

In-store purchases must be returned to an Express or Express Men retail store in the U.S. Returns will not be accepted by mail or at Express Factory Outlet stores. See “Returning to Stores” below to find an Express or Express Men retail store near you.

###### Express Factory Outlet Items

Please note, Express Factory Outlet stores cannot accept returns for non-Express Factory Outlet items purchased at Express or Express Men retail stores, or online at express.com.

In-store purchases must be returned to any Express Factory Outlet store in the U.S.

Returns will not be accepted by mail or at Express or Express Men retail stores. See “Returning to Stores” below to find an Express Factory Outlet store near you.

At this time, we don’t offer online exchanges. If you’d like to exchange a product, return it using our Returns process and then place a new order.

We’re happy to accept returns for items within 60 days of your purchase online. Orders shipped to addresses outside of the U.S. cannot be returned in stores and must be returned by mail.
