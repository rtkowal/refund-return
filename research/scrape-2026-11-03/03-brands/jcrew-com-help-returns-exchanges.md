# Returns & Exchanges | J.Crew

## 

**Start a Return:** [Click here](https://returns.narvar.com/jcrew/returns) or above to start a return for U.S. orders and generate a prepaid label (no printer required).

## 

*   When using our prepaid label $7.50 will be deducted from your refund.
*   Multiple orders can be returned in the same package if returns are initiated for each order and all return packing slips are included.
*   Online and phone purchases can also be returned in store, in accordance with our policy.
*   Purchases made in store must be returned to a store location**.**
*   Online purchases may be returned to a J.Crew store. J.Crew merchandise cannot be returned to J.Crew Factory locations. Refunds will be made in the form of the original payment, unless otherwise agreed.

## 

**Exchanges**

## 

We are pleased to now offer free exchanges. [Click here](https://returns.narvar.com/jcrew/returns) to start an exchange for U.S orders and generate a free exchange label. Exchanges are limited to online purchases of the same style number and same color but a different size of that item. Exchanges exclude final sale, marketplace items, and monogrammed items. Exchanges follow our normal return eligibility policy.

Unfortunately, we cannot offer exchanges for returned merchandise. Please return the item you wish to exchange for a refund in accordance with our return policy and place a new order for a replacement item.

## 

**U.S. Return Policy and Exchange Policy**

## 

J.Crew gladly accepts returns of unworn, unwashed, undamaged or defective merchandise purchased online, by phone or in stores for delivery that shipped to a U.S. address for a full refund or free exchange which are requested within 30 days of original purchase and include proof of purchase. Swimsuits without hygienic liners in place are ineligible for returns or exchanges. Return must be received and processed within 30 days to receive a refund to the original form of payment. You will receive an email confirmation when your return is complete.

For your convenience, online purchases may be returned to a J.Crew store. J.Crew merchandise cannot be returned to J.Crew Factory locations. Refunds will be made in the form of the original payment, unless we agree to credit a different payment type. Merchandise purchased with a gift card will be issued a refund on a new gift card.

To help ensure the integrity of our return policy, all returns, exchanges, refunds, store credits, and other post-purchase adjustments are subject to review. J.Crew reserves the right to limit, delay, or refuse a return, exchange, refund, or store credit if merchandise or proof of purchase does not meet our return requirements or if we reasonably suspect fraud, misuse, excessive returns, reseller activity, or other activity inconsistent with the purpose of this policy. As part of our evaluation, J.Crew may consider factors including, but not limited to, the condition of the merchandise, proof of purchase, return activity, and transaction history with J.Crew Group and our affiliated brands. We may request additional information to process in-store returns, including proof of purchase, a valid government-issued photo ID, or other information necessary to validate the transaction. For more information about how we process this data, please see our notice at collection at [https://www.jcrew.com/help/privacy-policy](https://www.jcrew.com/help/privacy-policy). J.Crew reserves the right to refuse future transactions, returns, or exchanges where we identify activity that we reasonably believe is fraudulent, excessive, or intended to circumvent our policies.

Items delivered by our Marketplace vendors must be returned directly to them by following the instructions enclosed in your package and will not be accepted at J.Crew stores or the distribution center. All monogrammed, personalized, lingerie, vintage, altered or final sale items cannot be returned or exchanged.

J.Crew is not responsible for non-J.Crew items shipped to us by mistake. Please ensure you include the correct item(s) in your package with the correct label. Unfortunately, we are unable to return or refund items sent in error or items that do not meet our return standards. Upon the return of an item, you will forfeit any rewards applied on that purchase. You will receive an email confirmation when your return is complete.

## 

**Returning a Gift**

## 

[Click here](https://returns.narvar.com/jcrew/returns) to start your return and generate a prepaid label (no printer required).

*   When using our prepaid label $7.50 will be deducted from your refund.
*   Once we receive your return, we will refund the purchase amount to a J.Crew E-gift Card.
*   In stores, requests to return or exchange merchandise received as a gift must be accompanied by a gift receipt. We will refund the purchase amount to a J.Crew Gift Card.

We are pleased to now offer free exchanges. [Click here](https://returns.narvar.com/jcrew/returns) to start an exchange for U.S orders and generate a free exchange label. Exchanges are limited to online purchases of the same style number and same color but a different size of that item. Exchanges exclude final sale, marketplace items, monogrammed items, and items paid for with loyalty points. Exchanges follow our normal return eligibility policy.  

Unfortunately, we cannot offer exchanges for returned merchandise. Please return the item you wish to exchange for a refund in accordance with our return policy and place a new order for a replacement item.  

**International Return and Exchange Policy**  
J.Crew gladly accepts returns or exchanges of merchandise purchased online within 30 days of original purchase. Final sale items cannot be returned or exchanged. If for any reason you are not satisfied with your international online purchase click [here](https://returns.eshopworld.com/eal) to initiate a return. Please allow an additional 48 hours after your package has been delivered before submitting your return request. Returns cannot be submitted until 48 hours post-delivery. If you choose to return an item, our e-commerce partner, ESW, will provide you with a refund of the original merchandise price and any duties and taxes originally paid on the item in the same currency and using the same exchange rate as your original order. Please note that you will still be responsible for any return shipping fees and original shipping charges.

J.Crew gladly accepts returns, in the country where purchased, of unworn, unwashed, undamaged or defective merchandise purchased in our retail stores for full refund or exchange within 30 days of original purchase. Requests for refund or exchange of merchandise purchased with cash must be accompanied by a government-issued ID and the original receipt. Refunds will be made in the form of the original payment, unless we agree to credit a different payment type. Requests to exchange merchandise received as a gift must be accompanied by a gift receipt. All monogrammed, personalized, lingerie, vintage, altered or final sale items cannot be returned or exchanged. Fine jewelry cannot be returned in stores; please contact us at +1 800 562 0258 to make arrangements for a return.

**Online and Phone Orders**  
J.Crew Factory accepts returns of unworn, unwashed, undamaged or defective merchandise purchased online, by phone or in stores for delivery that shipped to a U.S. address for a full refund or exchange within 30 days of original purchase. J.Crew Factory merchandise cannot be returned to a J.Crew retail location. Orders also cannot be returned to J.Crew Factory locations in Canada. Refunds will be made in the form of the original payment, unless we agree to credit a different payment type. Requests to exchange merchandise received as a gift must be accompanied by a gift receipt.

**Store Purchases**  
J.Crew Factory merchandise purchased in a J.Crew Factory store may be returned to any J.Crew Factory store location within 30 days of original purchase for refund or exchange. J.Crew Factory merchandise cannot be returned to a J.Crew retail location. Requests for refund or exchange of merchandise purchased with cash must be accompanied by a government-issued ID and the original receipt. Refunds will be made in the form of the original payment, unless we agree to credit a different payment type. Requests to exchange merchandise received as a gift must be accompanied by a gift receipt.
