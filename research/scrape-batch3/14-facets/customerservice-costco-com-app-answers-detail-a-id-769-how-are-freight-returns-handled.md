# How are freight returns handled?

Title

How are freight returns handled?

Body

If your item was delivered by a freight carrier, it will be picked up the same way it was delivered ([Curbside or Threshold or White Glove](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/1152)). There are a few exceptions, however: freight pickup does not include Special Order Kiosk, Custom Installed Programs, or Costco Warehouse Special Event purchases that may include delivery.

When the return is [initiated](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/6874), the carrier will contact you within 5-7 business days using the phone number listed on your order. You can set up a time for them to come by and pick up your order.

Once we have confirmed the order is picked up and in transit, we’ll process your refund. You’ll receive a confirmation email once we’ve issued your credit. We ask that you allow a few business days for your credit card company or bank to process the credit.
