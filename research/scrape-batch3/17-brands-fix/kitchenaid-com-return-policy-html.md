# Return Policy & Cancellations | KitchenAid

Our new return policy is in effect for all products and accessories.

## MAJOR APPLIANCES

###   
Returns within 15 days — No return fees  

Request your return within 15 days from the date of delivery for a product refund of products that are in new and unused condition (outside of initial testing by the delivery team). Simply call [1-866-808-9274](tel:18668089274) with your order number or product serial number handy.

Delivery, shipping and service fees will not be refunded.  
 

### Products Damaged Upon Delivery

(Damage including scratches, dents, chips, and other damage from defects in materials and/or workmanship.)

If you notice your product was delivered damaged, you can request a return, reorder, or exchange if your product is new and unused. If you want to repair the damage under the cosmetic warranty within 30 days call us at [1-866-808-9274](tel:18668089274)

*   For an exchange, you will not be charged any additional delivery, shipping or service fees. Depending on the new item’s price difference, you’ll receive a credit or charge to the credit card used to purchase the original product.
    
*   Payments made through Affirm, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Acima can only be return for a credit only if consumer is exchanging for a different model
    
*   For a return, you will receive a product refund, but delivery, shipping and service fees will not be refunded. Please call us to discuss the nature of the damage and confirm that you qualify for the product refund.
    
*   If you’re interested in a repair, just call the same number to schedule service.
    

###   
Major appliance returns for any other reason after 15 days

If you are Requesting a return between day 16 and day 30, an additional restocking fee of 20% of the product cost will be applied, with a $50 minimum. Original delivery, shipping and service fees will not be refunded. As long as the product is in new and unused condition (outside of initial testing by the delivery team). Please call us at [1-866-808-9274](tel:18668089274) to arrange for the return.

**NOTE**: If you're returning a product from Hawaii, your return will only be subject to a flat $50 restocking fee.

###   
After 30 Calendar Days

If the 30-day return window has passed, and your product is still under warranty, call us for warranty service or to schedule a repair. Fees may apply.

Call us at [1-866-808-9274](tel:18668089274) so we better understand how to help your situation.
