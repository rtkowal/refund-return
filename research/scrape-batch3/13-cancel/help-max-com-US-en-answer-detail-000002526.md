# Everything you need to know about cancelling your HBO Max subscription.

## How do I cancel HBO Max?

### Things to know

*   Your HBO Max subscription automatically renews until you cancel it.
    
*   Deleting the HBO Max app does not cancel your subscription.
    
*   Cancel your subscription before your next billing date to avoid further charges.
    

HBO Max subscriptions are available through [many providers](https://help.max.com/US-en/Answer/Detail/000002545). The cancellation steps vary depending on who bills you. If you know who bills you, skip to [Step 2](https://help.hbomax.com/US-en/answer/detail/000002526#step-2--cancel-your-subscription "Step 2: Cancel your subscription").

To find out how to cancel your Extra Member Add-On, go to [Extra members on HBO Max](https://help.max.com/US-en/Answer/Detail/000002598).

### Step 1: Find who bills you

Here's how to find who bills you for HBO Max:

1.  Do one of the following:
    
    *   **Web**: Go to [HBOMax.com/subscription](https://hbomax.com/subscription) and sign in (if needed). [Can't sign in?](https://help.max.com/US-en/Answer/Detail/000002552)
        
    *   **Phone, tablet, or computer**: In HBO Max, choose your profile and then **Subscription**.
        
    *   **TV device**: In HBO Max, choose the Settings icon ![cog, (lower left)](https://help.max.com/helpcenter/ByArticle/000002526/en/image/img-fe94d8458ccab28f7cd34eba88531e95.svg+xml) and then **Subscription**.
        
    
2.  Find who your subscription is billed through (top of screen).
    

You can also check your bank statements or ask your internet, mobile, or TV provider if they bill you for HBO Max.

### Step 2: Cancel your subscription

If you're billed through us (WarnerMedia), here's how to cancel your subscription:

1.  Go to [HBOMax.com/subscription](https://hbomax.com/subscription) and sign in (if needed). [Can't sign in?](https://help.max.com/US-en/Answer/Detail/000002552)
    
2.  Choose **Cancel Your Subscription**.
    
    **No cancel option?** Take note of who you're billed through (top of screen) and find them in the Third-party billing section below.
    
3.  Confirm your cancellation and take note of your expiration date.
    

You can stream until your subscription expires at the end of your billing period.

If you have the Extra Member Add-On, the add-on will be cancelled when your base plan expires. If you have remaining time on your Extra Member Add-On, you'll get a prorated refund for the remaining days on your add-on.

For more info, go to [Change a WarnerMedia-billed subscription](https://help.max.com/US-en/Answer/Detail/000002528).
