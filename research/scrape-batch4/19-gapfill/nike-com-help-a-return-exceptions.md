# Return Policy Exceptions | Nike Help

*   Apple Watch accessories must be returned within 14 days of an online order delivery or Nike store purchase.
*   Custom Jersey By You soccer jerseys cannot be returned.
*   Nike pre-orders and SNKRS Reserve orders cannot be returned.
*   Gift cards cannot be returned.
*   Items noted as nonreturnable cannot be returned.
*   Items purchased at Nike Clearance stores cannot be returned or exchanged and are not eligible for price adjustments. In addition, items purchased at other Nike stores or online cannot be returned to Nike Clearance stores.
*   Nike jewelry must be **unworn and in original condition with all original packaging** including the box, pouch, insert(s), and tag(s) to return.
*   Nike x LEGO® brick sets must be sealed and unopened to return.
*   Shipping fees are not refundable unless you receive an incorrect item.
*   Some Nike products have specific written guarantees and/or warranties—please check the label, hang tag, or warranty card for details and return information.
