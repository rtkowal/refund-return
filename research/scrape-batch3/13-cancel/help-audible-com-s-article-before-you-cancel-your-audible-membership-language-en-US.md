## How do I use my Audible credits before canceling?

**Any audiobook you get with your credits is yours to keep, even after you cancel.** Each credit lets you choose any title in our library, regardless of its retail price — whether it's $20, $30, or even $40. Use your remaining credits before your plan ends to build a library that's yours for good.

**Your cancellation credit checklist:**

*   Check your credit balance in your [Membership details](https://www.audible.com/account/overview).
*   Browse titles and use your credits on audiobooks you've been eyeing.
*   Remember: Any audiobook you buy with a credit is yours permanently — even if you cancel your subscription.

**Note**: [Credits](https://help.audible.com/s/article/learn-about-credits?language=en_US) expire 12 months after the issue date, or upon membership cancellation — whichever comes first, but **any** **purchas****e** **made** **with credits will stay in your library**. Credits received through the App Store or Google Play Store do not expire and will remain in your account after cancellation.

## How do I check my Audible billing and payment method?

Before canceling your Audible membership, confirm how your subscription is billed. If your membership is through the [Google Play Store](https://help.audible.com/s/article/manage-google-play-store-subscription?language=en_US) or [App Store](https://help.audible.com/s/article/manage-app-store-subscription?language=en_US), you'll need to cancel directly through their respective platforms — not through the Audible site.

**Your billing checklist:**

*   Verify your billing method in your [Membership details.](https://www.audible.com/account/overview)
*   If billed through the [Google Play Store](https://help.audible.com/s/article/manage-google-play-store-subscription?language=en_US) or [App Store](https://help.audible.com/s/article/manage-app-store-subscription?language=en_US), manage your Audible subscription in their respective app stores.
*   Review your next billing date so you know exactly when your Audible membership benefits end.

**Note**: Deleting the Audible app will not [cancel your membership](https://help.audible.com/s/article/cancel-membership?language=en_US).

## Can I pause my Audible membership instead of canceling?

Not ready to fully cancel? [Pausing your Audible membership](https://help.audible.com/s/article/pause-your-membership?language=en_US) lets you take a break from payments without losing your benefits permanently. You can pause eligible monthly plans for up to 90 days every 12 months.

**Your pause checklist:**

*   Check if your plan is eligible for pausing (annual plans are not eligible).
*   Visit [Pause your membership](https://help.audible.com/s/article/pause-your-membership?language=en_US) to learn more.

**Remember:** Pausing your membership keeps your existing credits active [until their original expiration dates](https://help.audible.com/s/article/learn-about-credits?language=en_US), and preserves your member discount. You can resume your membership anytime.

## What do I keep vs. what do I lose when I cancel Audible?

Understanding what happens after canceling your Audible membership helps you make an informed decision.

### What you keep after canceling Audible

|     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- |
|     | **What you keep** | **Details** |
| ✓   | Purchased audiobooks | Any audiobook you bought with a credit, credit card, or debit card is yours to keep and will be available in your library as long as it’s available on Audible. |
| ✓   | Unlimited re-downloads | You can download your purchased titles as many times as you want, even after cancellation. |
| ✓   | Your Audible account | Your account remains active. You can still purchase audiobooks at full price without a membership. |
| ✓   | App Store / Google Play credits | Credits received through these platforms do not expire and stay in your account after cancellation. |

### What you lose when you cancel Audible

|     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- |
|     | **What you lose** | **Details** |
| ✗   | Unused Audible credits | All remaining membership credits will be forfeited at the end of your final billing cycle. Credits expire 12 months after the issue date, or upon cancellation — whichever comes first. |
| ✗   | Included catalog access | Unlimited listening to the thousands of included audiobooks, podcasts, and Originals in the included catalog will no longer be available. Titles will show a lock icon in your Library. |
| ✗   | Member-only promotions | You will lose access to exclusive member discounts. Access to sales like BOGO offers will end. |
| ✗   | Standard plan monthly selections | Audible Standard members will lose access to their past monthly audiobook selections. These titles will also show a lock icon. |

**Important**: You will regain access to locked titles if you rejoin an Audible membership.

## What Audible plan options are available instead of canceling?

Before you cancel, consider switching to a plan that better fits your needs and budget. You can change your Audible plan without losing your existing credits — they'll remain available until their regular expiration date.

|     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Feature** | **Premium** | **Standard** |
| Monthly credits | Yes (1 or 2/mo) | —   |
| Annual credits | Yes (12 or 24/y) | —   |
| Included catalog access | Unlimited | —   |
| Exclusive discounts | Yes | —   |
| Monthly audiobook selection | —   | 1 title/month |
| Preexisting credit retention | Yes | Yes |
| Starting price | $14.95/month | $9.99/month |

You can switch your plan using self-service options on the Audible site. Visit Switch plans to explore your options, or chat with us — we'll be happy to guide you to the right plan.

**Note**: If your subscription is through the Google Play or App Store, visit Manage App Store subscription or Manage Google Play Store subscription for steps to switch membership.

## Still want to cancel your Audible membership?

Visit [Cancel membership](https://help.audible.com/s/article/cancel-membership) to proceed. After you cancel, you'll receive an email confirmation and your membership status on your Membership details page will reflect the change.
