# HSN

## At HSN, returns are easy!

It's so easy, here's all you need to know:

1\. Apply the label that came on your packing slip to the outside of the package.  
2\. Leave it in your mailbox or drop it off at a Post Office or UPS location.

That's it! Now, pick the option that is most convenient for you.
