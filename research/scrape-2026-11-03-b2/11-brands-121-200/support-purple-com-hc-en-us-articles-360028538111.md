# I want to return my Purple® Mattress, but I used the financing option. How does this process work? – Purple Innovation, LLC

We'll issue a refund for the entire purchase, minus any discounts, shipping, or transportation charges. Accrued interest is not refunded when financed through our financing partners. 

Contact Customer Care for any additional questions or to start your return! 888-848-2305.
