# Return & Exchange Policy | Urban Outfitters

![Returning Online Orders](https://images.ctfassets.net/q602vtcuu3w3/4HaFIXMUVyW4Yy8aICM4yg/2f9a300b3b97cfcb8e3a38d3202edc04/Topic_Returns_Exchanges__Icon_Returns_XS.jpg?w=706&q=80&fm=jpg&fl=progressive)

Returning & Exchanging Online Orders

Returning online orders is easy - return your eligible items by mail or take them to a UO store. To return by mail, [click here](https://www.urbanoutfitters.com/help/return-policy#order-inquiry) to start the process and print your return shipping label. A $5 restocking fee will be deducted from your refund for most mailed returns. To return or exchange your items in person for free, take your eligible items and packing slip or order confirmation email to your closest [UO store.](https://www.urbanoutfitters.com/stores) Please note, UO MRKT items cannot be returned to UO stores.

[Details](https://www.urbanoutfitters.com/help/return-policy#returning-and-exchanging-online-orders)

Returning In-Store Purchases

Items bought in one of our UO stores can be returned to any UO store. [Find a store](https://www.urbanoutfitters.com/stores) near you to check store hours and address info. Take your item and receipt to the store to return. Most returns will be credited back to your original form of payment within 1-3 business days.

[Find a Store](https://www.urbanoutfitters.com/stores)

Returning Furniture

Most UO Furniture items may be returned or exchanged by carrier within 30 days from the date of delivery. Please note, we do not accept furniture returns in our stores. Select furniture items are subject to a restocking fee if returned. We guarantee the quality of our furniture (free of manufacture defect) for up to one year from purchase. If you experience any defects, you may return your furniture purchase for a refund within one year from the purchase date.

[Details](https://www.urbanoutfitters.com/help/return-policy#furniture-return-policy)

**Holiday Return Policy  
We’re extending our return and exchange window for the holiday season. Purchases made from 10/1/2026 to 12/24/2026 may be returned for a refund to the original form of payment through 1/31/2027. See our full return policy below for more terms.**

Our Online Return Policy

Return or exchange any unused or defective merchandise by mail or at one of our US or Canada store locations. Returns made within 30 days of the order delivery date will be issued a refund to the original form of payment. Returns made after 30 days of the order delivery date will be refunded via an emailed e-merchandise credit. A restocking fee will be deducted from your refund for most mailed returns. Some items are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged. This includes made-to-order furniture, sale items purchased at an additional sale-on-sale discount, and furthered sale items ending in $0.95. Eligible UO MRKT items must be returned to the UO MRKT seller and cannot be returned to UrbanOutfitters.com or UO stores. After one year, we will no longer accept returns or exchanges. For online orders, the window for returns and exchanges will be determined by the delivery date of the shipped items.

To return items purchased on UrbanOutfitters.com, the UO app or by phone, follow the easy steps below.

Please note, return shipping labels are only available for orders shipping to the continental US and no additional postage is required. A $5 restocking fee will be deducted from your refund for most mailed returns. Please allow approximately 1-2 weeks for return processing. You will receive a refund notification from us when your return has been processed, and refunds will take an additional 1-3 business days to credit back to your original form of payment. Shipping fees and handling surcharges are non-refundable.

1\. Find Your Order Number  
If you have a UO account, sign in and go to your Order History. Select the order you would like to return and click View or Return/Exchange Items. Now you're ready to start the process! Don't have a UO account yet? Guest orders and orders received as gifts can be found by checking your [Order Status](https://www.urbanoutfitters.com/help/return-policy#order-inquiry).

[Start Your Return](https://www.urbanoutfitters.com/help/return-policy#order-inquiry)

2\. Print Your Return Shipping Label  
After you've created a return, follow the instructions to print your pre-paid return shipping label. When complete, you'll receive an email with tracking information and a copy of your return label just in case you need it.

3\. Attach Return Shipping Label  
Next, get your package ready to mail back to us. Add your items and tape your package securely. Make sure to remove or cover the original shipping label and attach your new return label to the package. You’re all set! Just schedule a pickup or drop off at any authorized facility for the designated carrier on your shipping label.

If you are returning items from multiple orders, please start a return for all items and include the receipt, packing list or order confirmation email from each order in your package.

Urban Outfitters is not responsible for any items not purchased from UO returned to us by mistake.

**International customer? Check out our [International Shipping page](https://www.urbanoutfitters.com/help/international-shipping) for info on how to return your item.**
