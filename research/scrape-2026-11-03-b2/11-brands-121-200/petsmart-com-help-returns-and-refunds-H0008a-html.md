# Return Policy

At PetSmart, your satisfaction is important to us. We've made our return process as simple and convenient as possible.

### Important Notes

*   Items eligible for returns are accepted within 60 days of purchase.
    
*   Treats Rewards points redeemed toward a purchase will be forfeited when the item is returned.
    
*   Returns will deduct any Treats Rewards points earned from the original purchase.
    
*   PetSmart reserves the right to limit returns regardless of receipt or proof of purchase.
    

## Returns for Online Purchases

### Return in Store (Free)

Bring the item in its original packaging, in new or sellable condition, and proof of purchase (e.g., order confirmation, order ID, or pack slips) to any PetSmart store within 60 days of purchase. Subject to exclusions below.

### Return by Mail

To initiate a return by mail, contact Customer Care at [1-888-839-9638](tel: 1-888-839-9638). Items must be sent in the original packaging, in new or sellable condition, within 60 days of purchase.

*   Use the return shipping label provided by PetSmart.
    
*   If you return an item without a PetSmart-issued label, all shipping costs are your responsibility.
    
*   Shipping charges are non-refundable.
    

### Refund Method for Online Returns

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| **Original Payment Method** | **Refund Method** |
| Credit or Debit Card | Original Card |
| Gift Card | E-Gift Card |
| Other non-credit card payment (ApplePay, Paypal, Afterpay, etc) | Original form of payment |

*   Refunds may take 3-5 days to appear on your credit card statement, or original payment methods transaction history.
    
*   Refunds made via e-gift card will be emailed to the same address used on the original order. E-gift cards can be used for future in-store or online purchases and cannot be redeemed for cash.
    

## Returns for In-Store Purchases

If you're not satisfied with a product purchased in a PetSmart store, bring the item in its original packaging or in new or sellable condition, with proof of purchase to any PetSmart location within 60 days. Subject to exceptions below.

### Important Notes

*   Returns older than 60 days are subject to approval and will be issued a store merchandise card for the most recent sale price upon approval.
    
*   Returns without proof of purchase are subject to approval and will be issued for the items most recent sale price. A valid government-issued ID will be required.
    

## Refund Methods

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| **Original Payment Method** | **Refund Method** |
| Cash | Cash |
| Check | Merchandise return card, or cash after a 10-day waiting period |
| Credit Card | Original Card |
| Debit Card | Cash |
| Gift Card | Merchandise return card |
| Other non-credit card payment (ApplePay, Paypal, Afterpay, etc) | Merchandise return card |

*   If the original payment card is unavailable, a merchandise return card may be issued.
    
*   Note: Merchandise return cards cannot be redeemed for cash.
    
*   For exchanges, any positive balance will be refunded using the original form of payment when applicable.
    
*   Refunds may take 3-5 days to appear on your credit card statement, or original payment methods transaction history.
    

## Return Exceptions

The following product types are ineligible for return:

*   Prescription medication products
    
*   Special order items (e.g.: personalized, embroidered, embossed, engraved products)
    

The following are not eligible for mail return, and must be returned to a PetSmart store:

*   In-store purchases
    
*   Buy online pickup in store orders
    
*   Same day delivery orders
    
*   Third party delivery app orders (Doordash, Instacart, Uber, Shipt, Skip, etc.)
    

A valid receipt is required to return or exchange the following items valued at $10 or more:

*   Electronic merchandise (including collars, fencing, and training products)
    
*   Grooming tools
    
*   Filters and pumps
    
*   Non-prescription pet medications and vitamins
    
*   ThunderShirts and calming products
    
*   Flea and tick products
    

A valid receipt is also required to return or exchange any PetSmart services purchased online.
