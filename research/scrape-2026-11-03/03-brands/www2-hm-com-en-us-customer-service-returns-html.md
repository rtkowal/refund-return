## Returns & Refunds

## Helpful information about returns

RETURN POLICY

You have 30 days to decide if an item is right for you. If you'd like to make a return or exchange, visit any H&M store in the United States (excluding Puerto Rico) with a valid receipt and tags attached, or register your return in the Returns Portal online.

Please note: A flat fee of $5.99 will be deducted from all mail-in returns of online purchases. In-store returns are always free.

For information about returning a restricted item, [click here](https://www2.hm.com/en_us/customer-service/transportation-restrictions.html).
