# Macy's Return Policy

**Shopping during the holidays?** Eligible purchases may qualify for an extended return period. See our [Extended Holiday Return Policy](https://www.macys.com/customer-service/articles/macys-holiday-2026-return-policy) for details and return dates.

* * *

 

##### How do I return an item?

If you’re not completely satisfied, most items can be returned within **30 days of delivery or purchase**.

* * *

##### How to Return

**In-store returns**

*   Items purchased in store must be returned to a Macy’s store
*   Returns are always free

**Online returns**

*   Items purchased online can be returned **in store or by mail**
*   **Return in store:** Always free
*   **Return by mail:** Free for Star Rewards members. Non-members pay a $9.99 return shipping fee (plus tax), which will be deducted from the refund.

* * *

##### Refunds

*   Refunds are issued to your **original form of payment**
*   Some items (like jewelry and watches) require an **inspection before refund is processed**
