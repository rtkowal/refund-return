# Returns Policy | Calvin Klein

**Need to return a gift? Our extended holiday return policy makes it easier. Purchases made between October 22, 2026 - December 24, 2026, can be returned through January 31, 2027.**

Online returns must be received by our warehouse within 30 days (45 days for My Calvin Rewards members) of the shipping confirmation date. Items must be unworn and tags must be attached. Once received by our warehouse, the return takes 3-5 business days to process. After processing, your refund can take up to 5 business days depending your card issuing bank.

**Return Processing Fee**

  
A flat rate cost of return shipping, **$4.95 USD**, will be deducted from your refund.  
If your refund is less than **$4.95 USD**, the return shipping fee will be charged to your original form of payment once your return is processed.  
 

**Credit**  
 

*   Allow **3-5** **business days** to process your return
*   Credit for the merchandise returned less a **$4.95 USD return processing fee** will be credited back to the original form of payment.
*   Allow **72 hours** for the credit to appear on your statement.
*   You will receive an email notification upon the completion of return processing.
