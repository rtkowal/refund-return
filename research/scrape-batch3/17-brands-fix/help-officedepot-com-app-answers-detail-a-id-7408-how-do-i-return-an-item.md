# How do I return an item

Returns can be made in store or by mail. For details on our return policy, review it [here](http://www.officedepot.com/cm/help/returns).

Return in store

If you purchased in store or online, just bring your item(s) with your Original Receipt to a checkout counter in any of our 1,000+ stores for your refund or exchange within the required timeframe. No receipt? No problem! Many items can still be returned or exchanged in store with a valid ID.

Return by mail

You will need an online account in order to process your return by mail. After you log in at officedepot.com, hover your mouse over Account, located at the top right side of the page. Select Order Tracking & History from the list of options. You will be directed to the My Orders page.

*   Find your order that includes the item you wish to return and click on the order number.
*   If the order is within the required return timeframe you will see the return option.
*   Click Create Return which is to the right of the order information.
*   Select the item you would like to return.
*   Print the FREE return label
*   Drop your item off at any carrier location
