   ![Let's get you taken care of](https://discounttire.scene7.com/is/image/discounttire/CUSTSERVreturn-policy_return-policy_hero_19?wid=300 "Iconic little old lady returning her tire")

If ever you're not satisfied with your purchase, bring it back and we will make it right. That is our promise. We stand behind our products and services and want you to be completely satisfied with every item you purchase.

## Returns and Exchanges

You can return item(s) at any of our Discount Tire stores. Simply bring the item(s) you'd like to return, along with your receipt and original form of payment. Returns will be handled on a case-by-case basis. Just reach out to one of our trusted experts at your local Discount Tire store, and we will work with you to make it right. [Find a local Discount Tire store.](https://www.discounttire.com/store-locator/)

If you choose to exchange a product for another, your account will be charged for the new item and credited for the return.

## Refunds

Eligible items may be returned and refunded to the original form of tender. If you paid by credit card, we'll credit your account within 14 business days. This will show on your next statement, depending on the issuing bank and/or billing cycle.

### What if my tire is damaged or defective?

* * *

Every tire we sell comes with a FREE prorated road hazard warranty, as well as a manufacturer's limited warranty. We also offer our Certificate for Repair, Refund, or Replacement program.

### Do I need a receipt from my purchase?

* * *

Your receipt or order number is the fastest way to help us locate your purchase. If you don't have a receipt, we should be able to find the purchase in other ways but we may require additional information.

* * *

### Do you take everything back?

* * *

We'll do our best to take care of you. However from time to time, we may be unable to accommodate a return.

* * *

### Is there a time limit? What if the item has endured minor wear and tear?

* * *

We will do our best to take care of our customers and deal with them fairly. There are occasions where we may not accept a return. There are no time limits for returns or exchanges.

* * *

### Are there restocking fees?

* * *

Not typically, however restocking fees may apply to products we have to custom order on your behalf. If you have any questions about this, please reach out to one of our trusted experts at your local Discount Tire store.
