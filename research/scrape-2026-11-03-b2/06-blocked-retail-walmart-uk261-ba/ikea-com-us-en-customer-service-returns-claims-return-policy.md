# How to return or exchange

### **In-store purchase**

Head to your local IKEA and enter through the Exchanges & returns entrance. Follow signage to the Exchanges & returns desk. Once you arrive, sign up to join the line.

You can start your return online [here](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/returns/).

If your items were delivered from the store and you need to schedule a pickup, please contact the Customer Support Center.

Do you need to return a small item that you recently purchased at the store? Please contact the Customer Support Center to return via FedEx at any of over 50,000 FedEx drop-off locations.

Returns are not accepted at IKEA Planning Studio or IKEA Pick Up Point locations.

### **In-store exchanges**

Exchange items at any IKEA store by bringing your merchandise, photo ID, receipt (or online order confirmation) and the card you used for the purchase.

Returns are not accepted at IKEA Planning Studio or IKEA Pick Up Point locations.

### **Online purchase**

Head to your local IKEA and enter through the Exchanges & returns entrance. Follow signage to the Exchanges & returns desk. Once you arrive, sign up to join the line. 

For a pickup, please contact the Customer Support Center.
