# Returns | Williams Sonoma

## Start Return Process (for UPS drop-off)

Begin the return process by giving us some details and we will create a UPS shipping label to bring to a UPS drop-off location. Pack your item(s) in the original manufacturer's box and use sufficient packing to prevent damage in transit.

Depending on the reason for return, you may be responsible for return shipping costs.

**Return item(s) must have originally been shipped to the United States (includes US Territories and APO/FPO) only.**
