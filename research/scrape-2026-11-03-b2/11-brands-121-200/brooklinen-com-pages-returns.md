# Returns & Exchanges | Brooklinen

We do our best to make you comfortable, because let’s face it, you’re the best. If you’re unhappy with your purchase, we’ll find a solution for you. Most of our products can be returned or exchanged within 365 days. Currently, Brooklinen offers exchanges on towels and robes. If your item qualifies, you can select a new color or size in the return portal.

All returns are subject to a $9.95 return fee deducted from your refund amount. Refunds can be issued to the original payment method or as a store credit.
