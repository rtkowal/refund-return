### Return Policy

Items purchased on Dillards.com that are eligible for return can be returned within 30 calendar days from the purchase date to the Internet Store location, 600 Carnahan Drive, Maumelle, AR 72113 ( see [Return By Mail](https://www.dillards.com/c/returns#returnByMailInfo) instructions below) or to any Dillard's store (excluding Clearance Centers). Electronic items, kid’s gear, and shoes must be returned in the original packing material from the manufacturer. **\* Holiday Return Policy.** Eligible items purchased between November 1st and December 11th can be returned until January 10th. If returned within 30 days of purchase, refunds go to the original payment method; after 30 days, refunds are issued on a Dillard's gift card. All purchases made outside these dates follow the standard 30-day return policy.

**Restrictions.** The following items are non-returnable and non-exchangeable: (1) clearance merchandise marked down 65% or more; (2) merchandise on sale with an additional discount, e.g. items marked down 25% plus an additional 30-50% discount for a limited time; and (3) personalized merchandise. The foregoing limitations on returns does not apply to merchandise that is damaged, defective or shipped in error.

**Requirements.** With respect to a returnable item purchased in our stores, Dillard’s will issue a refund for the item at any Dillard’s store if each of the following conditions has been met: (1) no more than 30 calendar days have passed from the date of purchase (unless the face of the receipt or a notice at point of sale indicates that a longer return period is available); (2) the merchandise is in its original, unused condition unless there is a manufacturer’s defect; (3) Purchases made in a store must include Dillard’s original proof of purchase label; and (4) all merchandise tags remain attached. Items must be returned in original packaging. Dillard's does not issue Price Adjustments.

**Refunds for Returned Merchandise.** For all eligible returns, Dillard’s will issue a refund in the form and amount of the original tender paid (including sales tax) or a Dillard’s merchandise credit, at the customer’s choice. Refunds for check purchases will have a ten (10) day waiting period before a cash or check refund is issued. Note – merchandise purchased using Afterpay and returned will be issued a Dillard's gift card or credited back to your Afterpay account for the purchase price. Original shipping fees will not be refunded

**Gift Returns.** If you wish to return merchandise purchased in a Dillard’s store that you received as a gift, you may return it to any Dillard’s store in exchange for a Dillard’s gift card or merchandise credit. If you wish to return merchandise purchased online that you received as a gift, you may mail the merchandise back to us in accordance with the instructions on the Customer Receipt, return it to any Dillard’s store in exchange for a Dillard’s gift card or merchandise credit. You will be responsible for any related shipping fees.

**Restocking Fee.** Dillard's reserves the right to charge a restocking fee for the return of certain products purchased on dillards.com. The restocking fee will be charged for this merchandise if it is returned by mail to our warehouse or to one of our store locations. If a product is subject to a restocking fee, the amount of the restocking fee will be noted on the Shopping Bag page prior to checkout. The restocking fee, plus any applicable taxes, will be deducted from your refunded amount. The restocking fee will be in addition to the $9.95 charged for use of a Dillard's return label.

#### In-Store Returns

1.  Bring any merchandise eligible for return to a [Dillard's location near you](https://www.dillards.com/stores) (excluding clearance centers), along with your receipt or proof of purchase.
    
    Proof of purchase or POP labels accompany every item sold to help us track sales and returns. Items purchased in store have the yellow label physically attached. For items purchased on Dillards.com POP information is included on the Order Status email sent when items are shipped or the email sent when items are ready for pick-up and picked up in-store or curbside. The same information can be found by accessing the Order Detail screen from the Order History page on Dillards.com and your Dillard’s Mobile app. Find the POP on your [Order History](https://www.dillards.com/webapp/wcs/stores/servlet/OrderStatusLoginChk?storeId=301&catalogId=301&langId=-1&purchaseHistory=true) or look up your [In-Store](https://www.dillards.com/webapp/wcs/stores/servlet/OrderStatusLoginChk?storeId=301&catalogId=301&langId=-1&orderStatus=false&storeReturn=true) or [Online](https://www.dillards.com/webapp/wcs/stores/servlet/OrderStatusLoginChk?storeId=301&catalogId=301&langId=-1&orderStatus=false&onlineReturn=true) purchase.
    
2.  Ask an associate for help or go to Customer Service for assistance.
    

#### Return By Mail

1.  Find the order on your [Order History](https://www.dillards.com/webapp/wcs/stores/servlet/OrderStatusLoginChk?storeId=301&catalogId=301&langId=-1&purchaseHistory=true) or look up your [In-Store](https://www.dillards.com/webapp/wcs/stores/servlet/OrderStatusLoginChk?storeId=301&catalogId=301&langId=-1&orderStatus=false&storeReturn=true) or [Online](https://www.dillards.com/webapp/wcs/stores/servlet/OrderStatusLoginChk?storeId=301&catalogId=301&langId=-1&orderStatus=false&onlineReturn=true) purchase.
    
2.  Click ‘Start Return’ and select the items you are sending back.
    
3.  Dillards offers a return label you can print from your computer and attach to the return package. The use of Dillards return label is $9.95.
    
4.  Re-pack the merchandise along with your receipt with Proof of Purchase. You may use the original box.
    
5.  Affix a return label to the box. Be sure to cover or remove any previous labels.
    
6.  If using the Dillard’s return label, drop your return at any USPS (U.S. Mail) location. Otherwise, please take your package to the carrier of your choice, in which case you will be responsible to pay for all applicable shipping fees.
    
    If you choose to ship without using the Dillard’s return label, please return the merchandise to the following address:
    
    Dillard's  
    600 Carnahan Drive  
    Maumelle, AR 72113
    
    If returning by mail, we recommend that you insure the package and ship via a traceable method.
