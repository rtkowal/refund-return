Jul 14, 2026

As a Hulu-billed subscriber, you can cancel at any time and easily renew a subscription if you choose to come back in the future.

**NOTE**: Canceling your Hulu subscription will not delete your Hulu account or the Disney account you use for other products and experiences from The Walt Disney Company, if applicable.

## **  
How do I cancel my Hulu subscription?**

If you’re billed through Hulu and would like to cancel your Hulu or bundle subscription, follow the steps below from a computer or mobile browser:

1.  Head to your [Account](https://secure.hulu.com/account) page on a computer or mobile browser
2.  Select **Cancel** under **Your Subscription**
3.  Next, you may be presented with the option to [pause your subscription](https://help.hulu.com/article/hulu-subscription-hold?language=en_US) — select **Continue to Cancel** if you’re not interested
4.  Select **Cancel Subscription**

### **What happens after canceling?**

After you’ve successfully canceled, the subscription status on your Account page should read: _"Your subscription will be canceled.”_  We’ll also send a confirmation email to the email address we have on file  — please keep this email for your records. 

 You’ll continue to have access to Hulu or your bundle plan until the end of your current billing cycle, but will not be charged moving forward. The same goes for free trial subscribers; if you cancel during your trial period, you'll continue to have access\*, but will not be charged once the trial has ended.

_  
\*Hulu + Live TV free trials will end immediately upon cancellation._

**  
TIP**: If you’d like to remove certain add-ons or change to a different plan, you can [manage your subscription](https://help.hulu.com/article/hulu-manage-subscription?language=en-US) without canceling Hulu.

##   
**How do I cancel my bundle plan with an existing subscription?** 

If you had an existing Disney+, ESPN+, or HBO Max subscription before you upgraded to a bundle plan, there are some additional things to be aware of when canceling your bundle plan.

**When you cancel your original subscription**, your subscription to your Disney+, Hulu, ESPN+ Bundle will continue uninterrupted, and you’ll continue to have access to Disney+, Hulu, and ESPN+ through that bundle plan. If you had been receiving a credit towards the monthly price of your Disney+, Hulu, ESPN+ Bundle plan for ESPN+, the credit will end when you cancel your original subscription.

 **When you cancel your Disney+, Hulu, ESPN+ Bundle**, your original Hulu, ESPN+, or third-party billed subscription will continue at the then-current price and terms (unless you also cancel the Hulu, ESPN+, or third-party billed subscription separately), but your access to the bundle will change according to the changes you made to your Disney+, Hulu, ESPN+ Bundle plan. Moving forward, you’ll continue to be billed for your original plan by the biller you signed up through.

  
**NOTE**: If you signed up for a **Disney+, Hulu, ESPN+ Bundle plan through Disney+**, please [manage your plan directly through Disney+](https://help.disneyplus.com/article/disneyplus-cancel) or [contact Disney+ Support](https://help.disneyplus.com/contact-us) for further guidance. If you signed up for a **Disney+, Hulu, HBO Max Bundle plan through HBO Max** please [manage your plan directly through HBO Max](https://help.hbomax.com/US-en/answer/detail/000002526) or [contact HBO Max support](https://help.hbomax.com/US-en/Home/Index) for further guidance.

## **Can I pause my subscription?**

Need a break from TV? No problemo! Hulu-billed subscribers may have the option to [pause a subscription for up to 12 weeks](https://help.hulu.com/article/hulu-subscription-hold?language=en_US).
