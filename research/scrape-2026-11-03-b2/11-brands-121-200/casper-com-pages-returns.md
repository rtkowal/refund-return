### **Pillows and Bedding Returns**

You can return your Casper item and get a full refund within 30 days of delivery or within 30 days of an in-store purchase, provided the merchandise is unused, and in its original packaging and condition.

![](https://casper.com/cdn/shop/files/Printer-icon.svg?v=1725394876&width=88)

Print Shipping Labels

[Contact us](https://casper.com/pages/contact "Contact Us") or click the order number in your original order confirmation email, to view and print your shipping label.

![](https://casper.com/cdn/shop/files/Returns-label-icon-2.svg?v=1725394989&width=88)

Prepare your package

Attach the return label to the outside of the box. If you don't have the original packaging, any sturdy box will do.

![](https://casper.com/cdn/shop/files/accolade-pcp-shipping_1.svg?v=1718636798&width=72)

Ship it at no cost

Drop your return package off at any UPS location. Sit back and relax.

### Furniture Returns

It’s important to test out all your Casper products in your own sleep environment. We offer a 30-night trial on our furniture products so that you can do just that. When you purchase your furniture with a paired mattress, we're happy to extend the trial to 100 nights to match that of your mattress. As long as your furniture is in donate-able condition, you can return it without the original packaging or box, even if it has been assembled.

### **Bundle Returns**

When you purchase a Casper Bundle, all of the items in the bundle will have the same trial period. If your bundle includes a mattress, the entire bundle will have a 100-night trial period.
