# 10 — E-E-A-T plan for a Poland-based operator on YMYL-adjacent topics

1. **Named reviewer** (owner decision 2): US-licensed attorney or consumer advocate, with a bio page, a bar number or credential link, and per-page `reviewed_by`/`reviewed_at`. Unreviewed pages are `noindex`.
2. **Named author/editor:** a real person with an editorial-policy page. Never imply a US law firm.
3. **Provenance visible on every fact:** "Source: {official URL} · last verified {date}". This comes straight from `snapshots` (02).
4. **Public update log** (`/updates/`) generated from `policy_changes`.
5. **Methodology page:** how we fetch, extract, verify and review, and what we don't do (we don't file claims or take money).
6. **"Not legal advice" placement:** one line near each generator and a full page. No modal walls.
7. **Claims registry** (08): every marketing claim maps to a test. Banned-word lint runs in CI.
8. **Transparency on location:** an About page that states the operator and country. Hiding it is worse than stating it.
9. **Entity signals:** Organization schema, consistent NAP, and later citations from consumer press, pitching the holiday-deadline table and the policy-change feed.
