### 90-Day Extended Return Policy

Any purchase made from February 1 at 11:59am ET can be returned for any reason for a refund of the purchase price, minus the S&H and Q Return Label fee, or exchanged for a free replacement of the same item in a different color or size. Food, personalized items, and gift cards are excluded. Restrictions may apply. QVC's 90-Day Extended Return Policy does not apply to Blowout Clearance Price items. QVC will only accept returns or exchanges for product defects or QVC errors. Call Customer Service at 888-345-5788 for such returns.

### Return an Item

Attach the Q Return label, which is found on most packing slips, to your package and follow the instructions, or [print a new return label](https://www.qvc.com/html/popups/ReturnsInformation.html#) by visiting Order Detail and clicking "Print Return Label."

### Arrange a Return Pickup

[Schedule a pickup now](https://www.qvc.com/USPSReturns.content.html) and USPS will come get your package from any address at no additional cost.

### Track Your Return Shipment

[View your return status](https://www.qvc.com/html/popups/ReturnsInformation.html#) by visiting Order Detail and clicking "Track Your Return."

### Find Previous Returns

[See a list of items you've returned](https://www.qvc.com/webapp/wcs/stores/servlet/OrderStatusView?langId=-1&storeId=10251&catalogId=10151&radFilter=4) on your Order Status page.

**If you still have a question**, check out our [Returns & Exchanges FAQs](https://www.qvc.com/content/information/returns-exchanges-faqs.html), send us an [email](https://www.qvc.com/CustomerServiceEmailView?storeId=10251&catalogId=10151&langId=-1), or give us a call at [888-345-5788](tel:+8883455788).
