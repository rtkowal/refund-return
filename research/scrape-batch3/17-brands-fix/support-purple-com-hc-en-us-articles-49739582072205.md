# What Is Purple's Return Policy? – Purple Innovation, LLC

*   [What Is Purple's Return Policy?](https://support.purple.com/hc/en-us/articles/49739582072205-What-Is-Purple-s-Return-Policy "What Is Purple's Return Policy?")

### Common Questions

*   What is your return policy?
*   Can I return my Purple product?
*   How long do I have to make a return?
*   What items are eligible for return?
*   What items are non-returnable?

## Mattress Returns

Eligible Purple mattresses can be returned within **100 calendar days of delivery**.

Requirements:

*   Complete the required **21-night adjustment period**
*   Mattress must be clean and undamaged
*   Law tag must remain attached

**One mattress exchange is allowed during the trial period.**

* * *

## Pillow Returns

Eligible Purple pillows may be returned within **30 calendar days of delivery**.

Requirements:

*   Pillow must be clean and undamaged

**Pillow exchanges are not available.**

* * *

## Seat Cushion Returns

Eligible Purple Seat Cushions may be returned within **30 calendar days of delivery**.

Requirements:

*   Cushion must be clean and undamaged

**Seat cushion exchanges are not available.**

* * *

## Bedding Returns

Eligible bedding products may be returned within **30 calendar days of delivery**.

Requirements:

*   Product must be clean and undamaged
*   Customer is responsible for return shipping costs

**Bedding exchanges are not available.**

* * *

## Non-Returnable Products

The following products are not eligible for returns, refunds, or exchanges:

*   Purple Squishy™ products
*   Freeform™ Extra Fill
*   Adjustable Bases
*   Smart Bases
*   Bed Frames
*   Platforms
*   Headboards
*   Flat Foundations

* * *

## Refund Information

After your return is processed and a refund is issued, it may take up to **14 business days** for the funds to appear in your account, depending on your financial institution.
