## Frequently asked questions

### How do I return my item in-store?

If you have a Walmart account, begin your return in the Walmart app or in your purchase history on [Walmart.com](https://www.walmart.com/).

1.  Select the items you’d like to return & a reason for the return.
2.  Choose “Return to store” to get a return barcode (eligible items will allow you to select “Curbside Return”).
3.  Review the return refund summary & submit your return.
4.  Check your email for confirmation. Bring this confirmation email with you to the store along with your item.

For curbside returns, simply park in a pickup parking spot & follow the instructions in the Walmart app.

Before coming to the store to begin your return, please remember the following:

*   Bring the item(s) you’d like to return, including all original packaging & accessories.
*   Please mark out any original shipping labels to avoid shipping errors or refund delays
*   Review the return refund summary & submit your return.
*   Bring any receipts for those items, if available.

* * *

### How do I start my return in the Walmart app?

If you have a Walmart account, begin your return in the Walmart app or in your purchase history on [Walmart.com](https://www.walmart.com/).

1.  Select the items you’d like to return & a reason for the return.
2.  Select return method.
3.  Review the return refund summary & submit your return.
4.  Check your email for confirmation. Bring this confirmation email with you to the store along with your item. For curbside returns, simply park in a pickup parking spot & follow the instructions in the Walmart app.

* * *

### Which items can be returned in-store?

Most items can be returned in-store except for freight, oversized, heavy, major appliances, and some Marketplace items (example: luxury goods, collectibles, certain hazmat items) within their eligible return window.

Have a Walmart account? You can upload your paper receipt to your Walmart app to make the in-store return process easier. With the app, use the scanner on your purchase history to scan your receipt.

* * *

### What if I don’t have a receipt?

We can attempt to look up store purchases with a debit/credit card or online orders with a phone number or email. Refunds processed back to a debit or credit card without a receipt may take up to 10 business days.

You can also [search for your store receipt here](https://www.walmart.com/receipt-lookup).

To return items without a receipt, customers must present a valid government-issued photo ID. If the refund verification process accepts the return, a Walmart gift card will be issued. 

In the future, you can save your receipts by scanning your paper receipt into the Walmart app. If you’re using the same payment method in the store that is saved to your account, your store purchases will automatically be added to your purchase history.

* * *

### What is a Walmart Balance?

Walmart Balance is a dedicated refund card in your wallet on [Walmart.com](https://www.walmart.com/). If your return is eligible, you will be given an option to choose Walmart Balance as the mode of refund. Refunds are processed within three hours once the return is complete or received.

You can view your Walmart Balance details in your [Wallet](https://www.walmart.com/wallet).

*   Your Walmart Balance can be used for purchases on [Walmart.com](https://www.walmart.com/), the Walmart app, or in Walmart stores.
*   Your Walmart Balance can be used as a payment method at checkout online or in stores via WMPay.

Walmart Balance restrictions:

*   Walmart Balance has the same restrictions as gift cards. See [gift cards terms & conditions](https://www.walmart.com/help/article/gift-card-terms-and-conditions/90f9e217278146898946c1d786bedea3).
*   You cannot purchase gift cards using Walmart Balance.
*   If you make a purchase using Walmart Balance, the refund for that purchase will go back to Walmart Balance only.
*   Your Walmart Balance is not redeemable for cash.

* * *

### Where is my shipping label, barcode or return instructions?

Return details, shipping labels or barcodes & instructions are provided in the app & are emailed to you. You can always find your return details on your order details page in your account.

Please cross out any original shipping labels to avoid shipping errors or refund delays.

NOTE: Only items from the same order can be processed in the same return. Refunds may be delayed if you combine items from multiple orders in a single box.

### What happens if my item was damaged?

Start a return in app through your purchase history & follow the instructions.

### How do I know if my return item contains hazardous materials?

Examples of hazardous materials include lithium and lead acid batteries, compressed gases (aerosols, propane, etc.), items containing flammable liquids (nail polish, perfume, fuel, etc.), some household chemicals, corrosive materials, etc.

For more information about what may be considered a hazardous material, [see the DOT guidelines](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/regulations/hazardous-materials/how-comply-federal-hazardous-materials-regulations).

* * *

### Do I need to return my item with its original battery?

Yes, items that include a rechargeable battery (e.g., tools, drills, lawn equipment, laptops, eBikes, etc.) must contain the original battery to qualify for a return.

* * *

### How should I prepare for my curbside return?

Start your return online in the app to know if your item is eligible for curbside returns. Items that are curbside eligible will show curbside as a return method.

Once curbside is selected, review the assigned store and select "Change" if you wish to return the item to a different location.

Once you’re ready, check in with the app to let us know you're on the way. Items do not need to boxed or packaged for curbside.

Park in a designated pickup spot. Using the app, tell us which pickup spot you are parked in and your car color, then an associate will come.

Show the curbside return barcode to the associate (this is not the same as the order barcode in your app). The associate will finalize your return.

If needed, you can finalize your return at the customer service desk inside the Walmart store.

* * *

### Do I need to erase personal information from an electronic device before returning it?

Yes, before returning items on which you've saved personal information, erase this information completely by following manufacturer's instructions. For further help on how to remove your personal information from the device, visit [this article from Federal Trade Commission Consumer Advise](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-remove-your-personal-information-you-get-rid-your-phone).

### When will I have to pay a restocking fee?

Some third\-party marketplace sellers may charge a restocking fee up to 20%. Some examples of this include luxury goods & items that are considered freight such as major appliances, heavy furniture, etc. For these items, please reference the seller's specific return policy & possible restocking fees.

* * *

### How should I return my gift item?

Our standard return policy applies to all gifts unless purchased during the holidays (October 1st through December 31st). After the return window has expired, you won’t be able to return your gift.

If the gift sender included your contact email when they purchased your gift, you’ll receive a delivery confirmation email which you can use to start a return.

Items on a gift receipt can be exchanged or refunded to an e-gift card or credited to the sender’s original method of payment.

If you didn’t receive a delivery confirmation email with a return link, you can return the item to a Walmart store near you and receive the refund on a Walmart gift card.

Items without a gift receipt can be exchanged or refunded to a gift card.

Alternatively, the gift sender can also start a return.

* * *

### How should I return my baby registry item?

Free returns are available for up to one year on eligible baby registry items. You can return the item by mail or in-store.

To start a baby registry return:

1.  Log in to your account & go to your registry landing page.  
    
2.  At the top of the page, select “See all registry items” then “Purchased.” Choose the item & reason for the return.
3.  Select how you would like to return the item & follow the instructions in the app.  
    
4.  Once the item is received, we will issue you a Walmart e-gift card with your refund amount.

If you are unable to access your baby registry, simply bring in the item(s), gift receipt & all original packaging & accessories to a Walmart store near you.

### How do I return my SNAP or WIC purchased item?

Eligible items can be returned for a cash refund/credit or gift card within 90 days only if the customer has a receipt showing the item was not purchased with WIC or SNAP.

*   If the customer has a receipt showing a purchase was made with WIC, the returned item may be exchanged for the same item.\* No cash refunds or credit/gift cards are allowed.
*   If the customer has a receipt showing a purchase was made with SNAP, returns may be refunded to the SNAP EBT account or exchanged for the same item. No cash refunds or credit/gift cards are allowed.
*   If the customer has a receipt showing a purchase was made without WIC or SNAP, items may be returned for cash refund/credit or Walmart gift card.
*   If the customer does not have a receipt showing a purchase was made with WIC or SNAP, eligible items may be exchanged for the same item. No cash refunds or credit/gift cards are allowed.

A WIC-eligible item is any item listed on the applicable state WIC-approved food list. A WIC-purchased item is any item on the applicable state WIC-approved food list purchased with a valid WIC food instrument (e.g., WIC voucher, EBT card, WIC CVV, etc).

A SNAP-eligible item is defined by the USDA as any food or food product for home consumption. It excludes specific items from being purchased with SNAP benefits. A SNAP-purchased item is any item purchased with a SNAP benefit.

\*Note: The customer must have formal documentation from the WIC agency to allow any exceptions.

* * *

### How do I return a large item?

Oversized furniture, major appliances, outdoor play, table games, treadmills & other large items considered freight purchased online & fulfilled by Walmart must be returned within 90 days by freight shipping. Just start a return in the app or contact Customer Care for assistance.

Items sold & shipped by a third-party marketplace seller must be returned within 30 days by contacting the seller to arrange freight shipping. Under some circumstances, you may not be refunded return shipping &/or you may be recharged a restocking fee up to 20%.

NOTE:

*   Major appliances must be returned within two days unless opened or un-installed
*   Freight/oversize/heavy items are not returnable to stores.
*   Items that require assembly (e.g., indoor/outdoor furniture, grills, bicycles, etc.) must be fully disassembled & repackaged prior to returning to a store or FedEx location for shipping (FedEx may decline service for unpackaged assembled items). If unable to disassemble & repackage a freight item, contact Customer Care for assistance.
*   Noncontiguous U.S. states & territories may have mailback restrictions.

* * *

### What if I have a recalled item?

Items Sold by Walmart: Some recalled items sold by Walmart are eligible for a return, refund, or store credit from Walmart, but for others, you may need to work directly with the manufacturer for a repair, replacement, or refund. WIC/SNAP restrictions apply.

Items Sold by Marketplace Sellers: Please follow the instructions in the recall notice, or contact, or work directly with the manufacturer or seller for a repair, replacement or refund.

More information about specific product recalls, including current recalls, can be found here: [Product Recalls](https://corporate.walmart.com/recalls).
