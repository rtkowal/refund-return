# How to Cancel Amazon Prime

Find more solutions

## Cancel Your Amazon Prime Membership

To cancel Amazon Prime, visit [Cancel Your Prime Membership](https://www.amazon.com/mm/pipeline/cancellation) and follow the prompts.

For more options on managing your Prime Membership, visit your [Prime Membership](https://www.amazon.com/mm/pipeline/cancellation?ref=help_endmembership) page.

If you need more support in canceling your Prime Membership, you can:

1.  Go to the [Customer Service Homepage](https://www.amazon.com/hz/contact-us/foresight/hubgateway)
2.  Select Something else under Can't find what you're looking for?
3.  Then select I need help with something else

*   Refund Eligibility:
    
    Paid members who haven't used their benefits are eligible for a full refund of the current membership period. Amazon will process the refund in three to five business days.
    
    For more information, visit the Cancel Membership section of the [Amazon Prime Terms and Conditions](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=G2B9L3YR7LR8J4XP).
    

*   Subscriptions:
    
    Subscriptions on Prime Video, like Paramount+, will continue once your Prime Membership ends.
    
    For more information, review your [Memberships and Subscriptions](https://www.amazon.com/hz5/yourmembershipsandsubscriptions?ref_=nav_AccountFlyout_digital_subscriptions).
    

*   Canceling Third-Party Memberships:
    
    If you signed up for Amazon Prime through Google Play using Android, cancel your subscription through [Google Subscription services](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid).
    
    If your Prime Membership is associated with another company's service, contact them to cancel your Prime.
