# How to manage subscriptions on PlayStation (US)

Learn how to see your active subscriptions, how to find out when a subscription expires, and how to cancel PlayStation®Plus or other subscriptions including GTA+, EA Play, and Ubisoft+ Classics. 

### How to check your PlayStation subscription payment plan online

1.  Sign in to [Account Management](https://www.playstation.com/acct/management).
2.  Select **Subscription**.  
    You see a list of the subscription services you’re currently subscribed to.
3.  Select a subscription to check the next payment date, or to [cancel](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/store/cancel-ps-store-subscription/#cancel) the subscription.

2.  Select **Account Information** > **Subscriptions**.

3.  Select a subscription to check the next payment date, or to [cancel](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/store/cancel-ps-store-subscription/#cancel) the subscription.

### How to cancel subscriptions on PlayStation

You can cancel PlayStation Plus (PS Plus), GTA+, EA Play, Ubisoft+ Classics, and other subscriptions on your PS5 console, PS4 console, in the PS App (mobile), or on the web.

Once you cancel your subscription, it’ll be deactivated starting from the next payment date.

1.  Select the **PlayStation Store** icon at the bottom of the screen.
2.  Select the menu icon from the top right of the screen and choose **Subscriptions Management**.
3.  Select **Cancel** below the subscription you’d like to cancel.

If you’d like to keep your subscription, select **Reactivate Subscription** to keep it active.

Please note, services may vary based on your country / region.
