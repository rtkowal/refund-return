# JCPenney Returns Policy

Peace of Mind for Gift Givers & Receivers– Holiday purchases can be returned through January 31st! Have a gift that doesn’t fit you? Not to worry – we can help. Most purchases made on or after October 1st may be returned through January 31st, 2027 with an original or gift receipt. JCPenney monitors return activity through the use of a third-party service and reserves the right to limit returns or exchanges with or without a receipt.

We gladly accept most returns at any JCPenney stores. Simply bring your item(s) and your receipt within 60 days of the purchase date and an associate will be happy to assist you.

Simply log in to your account (click the image above) and select the order made within the last 60 days. Click the “Start a Return” button and follow the prompts.

We are happy to exchange most merchandise purchased in the last 60 days at any JCPenney location.

Problem with an order or need help with a return? Our support staff will be happy to help. Tweet us @askJCP Call 1-800-322-1189

### **What is JCPenney’s Holiday Return Policy?**

Purchases made in store or at jcp.com on or after October 1st are eligible for a return or exchange with an original or gift receipt through January 31st. Refunds will be issued on the original form of payment or JCPenney Gift Card. Returns without a receipt may be eligible for a JCPenney Merchandise Return Voucher at the item’s lowest selling price in the last 45 days when accompanied by a valid ID. [Some Exclusions Apply.](https://www.jcpenney.com/m/customer-service/return-exceptions)

### **What is JCPenney’s return policy?**

Purchases made in store or at jcp.com are eligible for a return or exchange with a receipt within 60 days of the original purchase date. Refunds will be issued on the original form of payment or JCPenney Gift Card. Some exclusions apply. Returns without a receipt may be eligible for a JCPenney Merchandise Return Voucher at the item’s lowest selling price within the last 45 days when accompanied by a valid ID\*. [Some Exclusions Apply.](https://www.jcpenney.com/m/customer-service/return-exceptions)

### **What can’t be returned?**

Gift Cards, Donations, Monogrammed Items, Personalized Items, Altered Items, Body Jewelry, Face Masks, False/Fake Eyelashes, Ear Piercing Kits, Perishable Foods, Services, jcp.com Shipping & Handling Charges and Colorado Delivery Fees.

### **Do original tags have to be in place when returning merchandise?**

We do require that merchandise be returned with the original tags. Aside from Special Occasion Dresses and Men’s Tuxedos, the tags do not have to be in place on the merchandise.

### **How do I return an in-store purchase with a receipt?**

We gladly accept most returns at any of our JCPenney stores. Simply bring your item(s) along with your receipt within 60 days of the purchase date and an associate will be happy to assist with your return. [Some Exclusions Apply.](https://www.jcpenney.com/m/customer-service/return-exceptions)

### **What if I made the order on jcp.com?**

Items purchased at JCP.com are eligible for a return with a receipt within 60 days of the original purchase date. Refunds will be issued on the original form of payment or JCPenney Gift Card. Orders purchased with PayPal will be refunded to the original form of payment, or in some cases, issued by a check. [Some Exclusions Apply.](https://www.jcpenney.com/m/customer-service/return-exceptions)

A receipt for your jcp.com order could include:

*   Receipt received at pick up in-store
*   Order confirmation email
*   Order details within your Shopping History, if signed into JCP account
*   Packing slip shipped with package

Merchandise sold exclusively on JCP.com may have certain [exclusions that apply](https://www.jcpenney.com/m/customer-service/return-exceptions). Shipping and handling charges are non-refundable unless the merchandise delivered was the wrong item, is damaged, defective, or was shipped to a location other than the shipping address that was provided when ordered. Manufacturers’ warranties may apply in certain situations.

### **There are two ways you can return your jcp.com order:**

**At a JCPenney store:** Bring your order to one of our JCPenney stores. This is the quickest way to return most merchandise and receive a refund or store credit. Some exclusions apply. To return furniture/mattress items, call customer care at 1.800.322.1189.

**Online Returns by Mail:** Simply [log in to your account](https://www.jcpenney.com/orders/trackorders) and select the order with the item(s) you want to return. Click the “Start a Return” button and follow the prompts. If you do not have a JCPenney account, [click this link](https://www.jcpenney.com/orders/trackorders) to view your order and start a return. There is a return fee that will be subtracted from your refund amount. Credits can take up to 4 weeks based on shipping and processing time.

*   **Do we offer free returns at my local JCPenney store?**

### **How long does it take for a refund to appear on a JCPenney Credit Card account?**

Refunds to a JCPenney Credit Card typically take up to 4 business days to post to your account from the day the merchandise was received.

### **How long does it take for a refund to appear on Bank Card or Debit Card?**

For information about refunds to a third-party credit card, contact your financial institution. It typically takes up to 10 business days to post to your account from the day the merchandise was received.

### **What if I need to make a return for a purchase made with Buy Now Pay Later?**

With a valid receipt, refunds, partial and full, will be credited back to the Pay Later loan or a refund will be issued by Synchrony. For more information on timing and/or amount of refund, you can contact Synchrony at 844-373-4960.

### **Do you have a Price Match Guarantee?**

Yes, we offer our price match guarantee to ensure that you always find value in your purchase. Within 14 days after your purchase, if you find a lower, currently advertised price on an identical item at a competitor, just bring the ad to a JCPenney store and we’ll match that price and refund you the difference.\* Does not include: JCPenney Salon Services, Optical, Portrait, In-Home Custom Window Treatments or Services. For online purchases, call Customer Care at 1-800-322-1189. Purchases in Alaska and Puerto Rico are excluded.

### **What if I don‘t have a receipt?**

Returns without a receipt may be eligible for a JCPenney Merchandise Return Voucher at the item’s lowest selling price within the last 45 days. [Some Exclusions Apply.](https://www.jcpenney.com/m/customer-service/return-exceptions)

Valid photo identification is required for all non-receipted returns or exchanges. Information from the ID will be retained in a secured company-wide database to monitor the number of un-receipted returns. JCPenney accepts the following IDs for returns: U.S., Puerto Rican, Canadian and Mexican Driver’s License; U.S. State IDs; Puerto Rican IDs; Canadian Province IDs; U.S. Military IDs; U.S. Laser Visa; Passports; Matrícula Consular Card; Puerto Rican Voter Identification Card, and Mexican Voter Registration Card. JCPenney reserves the right to limit returns or exchanges with or without a valid receipt.

For purchases and returns made in the states of CT and MA: Customers must return any purchase within 60 days of the purchase date to receive a refund of the sales tax. Returns made after 60 days of the purchase date will receive the refund of the purchase price excluding the sales tax.

### **Returning Third-Party Marketplace Seller items?**

For products purchased from a Third-Party Marketplace Seller, “Third-Party Seller,” the following policy applies.

**Which Third-Party Seller products are eligible for returns?**

All marketplace items are eligible for returns, except for:

*   The items listed on the JCPenney exclusion list [here](https://www.jcpenney.com/m/customer-service/return-exceptions) or otherwise indicated on the listing as non-refundable.

**How to return a Third-Party Seller item?**

Orders may be returned within 30 days of receipt in the following two ways:

1.  **Return by Mail:**
    *   You may return an order by mail by logging into your account, selecting order history, and contacting the Third-Party Seller that sent you the item you wish to return.
    *   The Third-Party Seller will provide a free return shipping label for eligible returns. For bulky items, the Third-Party Seller will provide a suitable shipping solution, and a restocking fee may apply.
2.  **Return In-Store:**
    *   You may also return an item purchased through a Third-Party Seller at any JCPenney store, provided the item can fit in a box size of 24" x 14" x 10" or a polybag 33" x 32", is less than 40 lbs., and is not otherwise excluded from in-store returns, including oversized, hazardous, or seller-direct return-only items.
    *   We will then return the item to the Third-Party Seller for you at no cost.

**Condition and refunds for Third-Party Seller returns.**

*   All returned items must be in their original packaging, unused, in resalable, as-new condition, and include all original tags, components, and documentation, and be properly disassembled, if applicable.
*   Once the Third-Party Seller has received the returned item and inspects it, and the item passed inspection, your refund will be processed and will take up to 4 business days: however, it may take additional time for your financial institution to post the refund to your account.
*   Refunds will be issued to the original form of payment, unless otherwise required by applicable law.
*   You will receive a full refund for the item, subject to:
    *   **Item Condition:** If the returned item is not in acceptable condition, your refund may be reduced by up to 50% as a restocking fee, as determined based on the item's condition at the time of return.
    *   **Bulky Items:** If the item is bulky and requires a special return delivery service, your refund may be reduced by up to $80, as determined based on the cost of return logistics and handling.

**Note:**

*   If you received a product that is or becomes subject to a warranty claim, such as a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact the Third-Party Seller to receive a satisfactory resolution, which may include a refund, replacement, or exchange.
*   If at any time you are not satisfied with the Third-Party Seller's support for either a return or warranty claim, please contact us for assistance [here](https://www.jcpenney.com/m/customer-service).
