# Cancel Your Kindle+ Subscription - Amazon Customer Service

Find more solutions

Follow these steps to cancel your Kindle+membership.

Want to cancel your Kindle+ Membership? Do it from here:

**Important:** You’ll not receive a partial refund for unused months for a prepaid (gift or bundle) Kindle+ Membership.

**If you signed up through Amazon**:

1.  Go to [Cancel Kindle+](https://www.amazon.com/kindle-dbs/hz/cancel) and sign into your Amazon account.
2.  Select Cancel membership.
    
    Your membership remains active until the next billing date. After the billing date passes, you lose access to content that you have checked out.
    

**Tip:**

*   You can also choose to pause your monthly subscription for a period of 1 month, instead of cancelling. The pause becomes effective at the end of the billing cycle.
*   Your subscription resumes automatically, or you can resume it anytime while paused.
*   You can't pause your subscription while on a free trial or promotional period.

If you pause your subscription, you can resume it from [Kindle+ Central](https://www.amazon.com/kindle-dbs/ku/ku-central).

**If you signed up through Google Play Billing**:

1.  Open the **Google Play Store** app on your Android device.
2.  Go to **Payments & Subscriptions**.
3.  Select **Subscriptions**.

**Note:** Your membership remains active until the next billing date. After the billing date passes, you lose access to content that you have checked out.
