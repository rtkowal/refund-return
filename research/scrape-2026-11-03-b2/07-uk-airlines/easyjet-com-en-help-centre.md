# Help Centre | easyJet

Important update – new border checks may affect waiting times at EU airports

**Border checks may affect waiting times at EU airports**

Airports across Europe may experience longer waiting times due to the European Entry/Exit System (EES). As part of these additional passport control checks, you may be required to provide biometric information, including a facial scan and fingerprints, either before departure or when arriving at your destination.

For departures, passport checks often take place after the initial departure lounge, after security, before you reach your gate, so please pass through security to reach passport control as soon as you can. Once you're through, keep an eye on the screens and the easyJet app, and head to your gate as soon as it's announced. If you are travelling with hold luggage, plan your arrival around your airport's Bag Drop opening and closing times.

For arrivals, these additional checks could also increase the time it takes to get through the airport when you land, so we recommend allowing extra time for onward travel arrangements and airport transfers.

**Tips for travelling**

*   Keep your travel documents easily accessible to help speed up processing
*   Proceed through security and passport control as early as possible to avoid delays
*   Head to your gate or boarding area as soon as it is announced
*   Download the easyJet app for live flight updates, including gate information and boarding announcements
