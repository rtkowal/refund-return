# Our Policy

**Need to return a gift? Our extended holiday return policy makes it easier. Purchases made between October 22, 2026 - December 24, 2026, can be returned through January 31, 2027.**

**RETURNS**

  
Online returns must be received by our warehouse within **30 days** (45 days for Hilfiger Club members) of the shipping confirmation date. Once received by our warehouse, the return takes 3-5 business days to process. After processing, your refund can take up to 5 business days depending on your card issuing bank.

  
**Please note: a flat rate return shipping fee of $4.95 will be deducted from your refund.**

Apparel must be unworn with original tags attached. Footwear must be unworn and in its original shoebox. We reserve the right to deny a refund if returned merchandise does not meet our requirements.

**Final sale items are not eligible for a return, refund or credit.**
