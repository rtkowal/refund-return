# Refunds and Returns | Customer Support ALDI

How you request a refund or replacement depends on how you made your purchase.

**In-Store Purchases**

If you purchased your item in an ALDI store: Our [Twice as Nice Guarantee](https://help.aldi.us/faqs/article/Twice-As-Nice-Guarantee) may apply to eligible items.

**Online Purchases**

If you placed your order online through aldi.us or a third-party delivery such as Instacart, DoorDash or Uber Eats, your order is fulfilled and supported by the service provider. Please see [Online Grocery Order Support - Start Here](https://help.aldi.us/faqs/article/ALDI-Partnerships) for assistance.

  
ALDI Twice as Nice Guarantee applies to in-store purchases only.  For purchases made online, please see policy [here.](https://help.aldi.us/faqs/article/Online-Orders---Refunds-and-Returns)
