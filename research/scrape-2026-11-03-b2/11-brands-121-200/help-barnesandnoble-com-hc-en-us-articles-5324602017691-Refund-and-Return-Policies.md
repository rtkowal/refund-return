# Refund and Return Policies – Barnes & Noble

Summary - Barnes & Noble allows returns within 30 days for items bought online or in-store, requiring original condition and proof of purchase. NOOK devices can be returned within 14 days with authorization. Opened shrink-wrapped products, magazines, digital content, and bulk orders are non-returnable. Refunds exclude shipping fees, and store credits apply for some returns.

Please review the following choices carefully, refunds and returns can only be processed when eligible items are returned using the appropriate method. If your return is shipped or brought to a store after the timeframes indicated below, we regret that we cannot issue a refund or send the merchandise back to you. 

Click the link in the table below to view the return policy that best applies to your purchase:

### **2026 Holiday Return Policy** 

As in previous years, we have extended our return policy for the holidays. Purchases made between November 1st and December 31st, 2026 are refundable or exchangeable through January 31st, 2027, including sealed or unsealed NOOK devices.

NOOK Protect warranties can be added up to NOOK devices up to 60 days after the original purchase date for purchases made between November 1st and December 31st, 2026.

### **Returning Items Bought from bn.com**

If you're not satisfied with your purchase, you may return it for a refund. We will issue a refund for the purchase price of the item plus any applicable taxes to your original form of payment for items returned within 30 days after the delivery date. Refunds are processed within 24 hours after being requested, but may take several business days to appear on your online statement depending on your card issuer's internal processes.

All returned items must be in their original condition; shrink-wrapped or sealed products must be unopened. Shipping fees are not refunded.  [Click here](https://help.barnesandnoble.com/hc/en-us/articles/5324602017691-Refund-and-Return-Policies#Exceptions) for exceptions.

There are two options for returning items purchased on bn.com:

_**1\. Bring Your Return to a Barnes & Noble Store**_

You can bring the item and your packing slip, Order Confirmation e-mail, or Billing Summary email to any Barnes & Noble store with your original form of payment.  

**Please Note:** If you used ShopPay or PayPal to complete your order, the store will provide a store credit in the form of Barnes & Noble Gift Card or in-store credit. The store will also issue a store credit for returns of textbook purchases.

[Click here](http://stores.barnesandnoble.com/) to look up your local store's contact information.

_**2\. Ship Your Return Back to Us**_

You can ship the item to the Returns Department. 

Most shipments do not come with a packing slip. If your packing slip is unavailable, please print out the _Shipping Confirmation_ email you received after the item(s) shipped. Include that printout in the box and write your **Order Number** on the **outside** of the box. Without a packing slip or shipping confirmation printout, we cannot process the return and you will not receive your refund.

If you are sending us a return because you were not sent the item(s) you ordered, indicate on the Packing Slip/Shipping Confirmation the item(s) that you were supposed to receive so the warehouse knows how to process the return.

  
Returns should be sent to: 

**Barnes & Noble**  
**Returns Department**

**ORDER #: (Insert Your Order Number)**  
**1 Barnes and Noble Way**  
**Monroe Township, NJ 08831**

**Please Note:** A packing slip/confirmation email is not a prepaid label. You will be required to pay for return shipping. Shipping charges are non-refundable.

If you are returning a gifted item that was purchased on bn.com, a Barnes & Noble Gift Card will be issued for the purchase price of the item within 60 days of purchase.

_**Orders Shipping to a Correctional Facility**_

Correctional facilities have specific rules and restrictions regarding the items they can receive. Before placing an order, please ensure you understand and comply with the facility’s shipping requirements and restrictions. We cannot accommodate refunds or replacements for orders that are rejected or not delivered because they did not meet the facility’s requirements.

### Returning Items Bought in Barnes & Noble Stores

If you're not satisfied with your purchase, a full refund for the original purchase price, including any applicable taxes, will be issued in store for returns of new and unread/unopened merchandise made within 30 days of purchase with a store sales receipt with the below qualifications:

 - Eligible refunds will generally be made to your original form of tender. Store credit in the form of an electronic gift card will be issued if the original form of payment is not available.

 - For returns with a gift receipt, a store credit will be issued in the form of an electronic gift card for the purchase price within 60 days of purchase. A store credit will also be issued for all textbook returns.

 - Items purchased as part of a Buy X, Get Y Free offer are available for exchange only, unless all items purchased as part of the offer are returned, in which case such items are available for a refund (within 30 days). Exchanges of the items sold at no cost are available only for items of equal or lesser value than the original cost of such item.

 - Returns or exchanges will not be permitted after 30 days or without receipt or for product not carried by Barnes & Noble.

 - Items purchased from a Barnes & Noble store must be returned to a Barnes & Noble store, but you are not required to return it at the same Barnes & Noble store where the purchase was originally made (Ex: an item purchased in a New York City store can be returned to a store in New Jersey as long as it is within the return timeframe).

Return Exceptions

### Returning NOOK Devices

You can return your undamaged NOOK within 14 days of receipt from bn.com, with either of the following options:  
 

A.) [Click here](https://help.barnesandnoble.com/hc/en-us/articles/5202053403675-Contact-Us) to contact us for a return authorization,  
OR  
B.) Return it to a Barnes & Noble store. - [Stores near you](http://stores.barnesandnoble.com/)  
 

**Please Note:** If you do not call for a return authorization number and send your NOOK to the wrong return address, no refund will be provided and your NOOK will not be returned to you. Shipping charges are non-refundable.

If you purchased your NOOK in a Barnes & Noble store, you will have to return it to a Barnes & Noble store. There is no option to send store purchases back to our warehouse. You can return it to any Barnes & Noble store, not only the one where you originally purchased it. 

NOOKs purchased from other retailers or sellers are returnable only to the retailer or seller from which they were purchased pursuant to such retailer’s or seller’s return policy.

Defective NOOKs may be exchanged at a store or by [contacting Customer Service](https://help.barnesandnoble.com/hc/en-us/articles/5202053403675-Contact-Us), in accordance with the applicable warranty.

### Items That Cannot Be Returned

Shrink-wrapped or sealed product that has been opened and/or removed from original packaging may not be returned, unless it is defective, in which case it can be exchanged only for the same product. For assistance with an exchange for a defective item, visit a Barnes & Noble store or [contacting Customer Service](https://help.barnesandnoble.com/hc/en-us/articles/5202053403675-Contact-Us).

Magazines, newspapers, used books and shipping fees are not returnable or exchangeable.

Digital content, including eBooks, audiobooks, digital magazines and newspapers, Access Codes, gift cards, and shrink-wrapped items that have been opened, are not returnable. However, if you are having compatibility or quality issues with your content, please [contact us](https://help.barnesandnoble.com/hc/en-us/articles/5202053403675) for assistance.

Memberships and subscriptions have additional eligibility rules.

 [- Barnes & Noble Membership Terms & Conditions](https://www.barnesandnoble.com/h/membership-terms-conditions)

 - Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time; you may receive a full or partial refund for any unused/unredeemed portion of the subscription.

Bulk orders of 50 or more copies are non-returnable.
