# Returns Policy | Dyson

## Dyson Direct Return Policy

###### **Dyson Machines**

To return a machine purchased from Dyson.com for a full refund, you must return the machine within 30 days from the delivery date. For machine purchases made at a Dyson Demo Store or Dyson Service Center, the return must be made within 30 days from the purchase date. Dyson Direct will pay for eligible standard return shipping.

Different return periods or conditions apply to certain products, as described below.

###### **Dyson Headphones**

This policy excludes wearable devices such as Dyson Zone and Dyson OnTrac. Dyson headphones have a 14-day refund policy from the delivery date. If purchased at a Dyson Demo Store or Dyson Service Center, the return must be made within 14 days from the purchase date. Dyson Direct will pay for eligible standard return shipping.

###### **Dyson Oral Care Devices**

Dyson Oral Care Devices may be returned for a change of mind within 30 days from the delivery date or, if purchased at a Dyson Demo Store or Dyson Service Center, within 30 days from the purchase date. To qualify for a change-of-mind return, the product must remain unopened and unused.

A Dyson Oral Care Device is considered opened if the hygiene seal has been broken.

To request a return, contact Dyson at 1-866-MY-DYSON (1-866-693-9766). Do not send or bring the product to Dyson before receiving return instructions.

Once received, Dyson will inspect the product to confirm that it meets the applicable return conditions. Products that do not meet the unopened and unused conditions are not eligible for a change-of-mind refund.

The unopened and unused restriction applies only to change-of-mind returns. It does not affect the support or remedies available if the product is defective, damaged, unsafe, incorrectly supplied, or covered by an applicable Dyson warranty or legal requirement.  
 

###### **Personalized Accessories and Accompanying Machines**

  
Personalized accessories and accompanying machines cannot be returned, refunded, or exchanged once your order is placed. If your personalized accessory or accompanying machine arrives damaged, defective, or the personalization was applied incorrectly, please contact Dyson [Support](https://www.dyson.com/support/contact-us "support").

###### **Refurbished and Final Sale Machines**

Dyson Outlet refurbished machines and Final Sale machines, including the first-generation Dyson Airwrap, are final sale and are not eligible for change-of-mind returns. They remain eligible for applicable warranty service.

If your machine arrived damaged or you received the wrong product, please contact us.

###### **Product Modifications**

Intentional cosmetic decoration or modification that may impact a machine’s resale, refurbishment, restoration or usability will void the terms of the Dyson return policy.

###### **How to Return a Machine**

If you wish to return any machine purchased from Dyson Direct, please contact our toll-free Contact Center at 1-866-MY-DYSON (1-866-693-9766) or submit the above form prior to returning your merchandise.

To receive a full refund for a machine purchased from Dyson Direct, please follow these three steps:

1.  Call the Contact Center to initiate your return or submit  the online return form to identify the correct return shipping address and method.
2.  Package the item or items to be returned in the original Dyson packaging.
3.  Send your returned item or items to Dyson using the authorized prepaid shipping method within the applicable return period. Unauthorized COD shipments will not be accepted.

For Dyson Oral Care Devices, contact the Contact Center before returning the product. The product must remain unopened and unused to qualify for a change-of-mind return.

###### **Miscellaneous Items Sent to Dyson**

If you send miscellaneous items to Dyson, please notify us as soon as possible. If you have not claimed your item or items and paid any associated charges within 60 days after Dyson receives them, Dyson reserves the right to consider the items abandoned.

Where possible, Dyson will provide notice using the email address on file. If Dyson does not hear from you within 60 days after receiving the items, Dyson reserves the right to dispose of them in accordance with applicable law. Dyson reserves its statutory and other lawful liens for unpaid charges.

###### **Counterfeit Products**

If a product is returned to Dyson and, upon inspection, Dyson determines that the product is not a genuine Dyson product (i.e., it is counterfeit), the product will not be eligible for a refund, exchange, credit, or any other return-related remedy under this Returns Policy.

You acknowledge and accept that, in its sole discretion, Dyson may:

*   return the counterfeit product to you; or
*   permanently retain or destroy the counterfeit product and provide you with a letter confirming that the product has been determined to be counterfeit, which you may present to the seller of the product, payment provider, or credit card issuer when seeking reimbursement.

Counterfeit products may be unsafe for use. Dyson reserves the right to retain or destroy counterfeit products in order to: (a) prevent personal injury or property damage; (b) prevent the resale or further distribution of counterfeit products; and (c) protect the Dyson brand, consumers, and reputation.

Further information regarding counterfeit products is available at https://www.dyson.com/campaigns/counterfeit-machines.

###### **Refund Processing**

Please allow 4–8 weeks for return processing. Eligible machines returned to Dyson Direct will receive a full refund to the original payment method, and Dyson Direct will pay for eligible standard return shipping.

Dyson reserves the right to reject a return request in cases of suspected or actual fraud or abuse of the return policy.

###### **Contact Us**

If you have questions, you can contact us by email or phone:

*   **questions@dyson.com** – Our Digital Support team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Most inquiries are handled within two hours.
*   **1-866-MY-DYSON (1-866-693-9766)** – Our Helpline is available from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Monday through Friday and from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Saturday. The Helpline is closed Sunday.

###### **Money-Back Guarantee for Formulations**

At Dyson, we stand behind our products and quality controls. If you are not satisfied with an eligible Dyson formulation product purchased from the Dyson website, you may request a refund or replacement within 60 days from the date of purchase. The Guarantee is limited to original end-user purchasers in the United States.

The formulation Money-Back Guarantee is separate from the return conditions applicable to Dyson Oral Care Devices.

###### **How to Claim the Guarantee**

To submit a Guarantee request, call our toll-free Contact Center at 1-866-MY-DYSON (1-866-693-9766). Please have your Dyson.com order information and the reason for your dissatisfaction available.

Dyson may ask you to submit photographs of the product or its packaging, mail the unused portion of the product to Dyson, or provide other information needed to process your request.

You must submit your Guarantee request within 60 days from the original purchase date. You are responsible for any costs incurred in mailing the product and proof of purchase.

###### **Guarantee Guidelines**

Dyson reserves the right to verify the information provided, require valid proof of purchase, and deny a Guarantee request in cases of suspected fraud or where Dyson concludes that the purchaser has abused the Guarantee.

Dyson may amend or terminate the Guarantee at any time without notice.
