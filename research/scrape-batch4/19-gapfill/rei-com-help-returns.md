# Return Policy & Instructions | REI Co-op

### 100% satisfaction guaranteed

We stand behind everything we sell. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you can return it for a replacement or refund—[with a few exceptions](https://www.rei.com/help/returns#return-policy-exceptions)—within one (1) year of the purchase date if you are an [REI Co-op Member](https://www.rei.com/membership) or within 90 days of your purchase if you are not a member.

| --- | --- | --- |
| All eligible items (includes e-bikes)<br><br>[See our return policy exceptions list](https://www.rei.com/help/returns#return-policy-exceptions) | 365 days | 90 days |
| Outdoor electronics | 90 days | 90 days |
| In-store Re/Supply used gear purchases, REI Co-op Memberships, lift tickets, passes and gift cards | Final sale | Final sale |

REI purchases of new items can be returned by mail or at any of [our REI stores](https://www.rei.com/map/store) with [proof of purchase](https://www.rei.com/help/returns#faq-0), regardless of whether you bought the item online, in a store or by phone. (Please note: Re/Supply stores are unable to accept returns of any kind.)

For information on the warranties covering products sold by REI and warranty returns, [see the REI Terms of Sale (Part Two)](https://www.rei.com/help/terms-of-use#Part_Two_Terms_of_Sale).

## Return to a store

Bring your item with its original product packaging and tags (if available), as well as [proof of purchase](https://www.rei.com/help/returns#faq-0), to the customer service desk at any of our [REI stores](https://www.rei.com/map/store) for a free and easy return or exchange. Re/Supply stores are unable to accept returns of any kind.

**Please note:** Purchases of $500 or more made with cash will be refunded by check.

[Return to Returns & Exchanges page navigation](https://www.rei.com/help/returns#in-page-nav)

## Return by mail

Whether you need to return items purchased online or in a retail store, you can begin the mail-in return process online. See [exceptions for mail-in returns](https://www.rei.com/help/returns#exceptions-for-mail-in-returns).

### Get your shipping label

Start your mail-in return online to get a shipping label. With an REI shipping label, you can drop off your return at any [UPS locations](https://www.ups.com/dropoff) or mail it directly from your home.

**No printer?** We can provide a QR code for fast paperless drop-off.

**Please note:** $7.99 will be deducted from your refund to cover return shipping for standard size/weight packages when your return is mailed using the prepaid shipping label provided. See additional information on:

FAQ for [returning oversize items](https://www.rei.com/help/returns#faq-3)

FAQ for [returning multiple orders in a single box](https://www.rei.com/help/returns#faq-5)

FAQ about [return shipping fees](https://www.rei.com/help/returns#faq-12)

#### Online, phone and in-store pickup orders

Start a mail-in return for orders placed online, over the phone or for in-store pickup. If you have an online account and are logged in, visit Purchase History, view the order details and select “Start a return” for eligible items in that order. Another option is to use the order number and billing ZIP code (found in your order confirmation email) to manually look up the order and start a return. 

[Start return via Purchase History](https://www.rei.com/purchase-history)[Start return with manual lookup](https://returns.narvar.com/rei-co-op/returns)

#### In-store purchases

For all in-store purchases, start a mail-in return via the button below with information from your store receipt.

[Return in-store purchases](https://returns.narvar.com/rei/returns)

**Please note:** Store purchases returned by mail will be refunded by check, including any portion purchased with Total REI Rewards. We'll email once we've issued a check—typically within 5 business days after we receive your package.

See [Gift returns](https://www.rei.com/help/returns#gift-returns) for instructions to return by mail.

Unfortunately, we are not able to offer direct exchanges by mail. See [Exchanges](https://www.rei.com/help/returns#exchanges) for more information.

## Gift returns

Returning a gift to any of our stores is easy. Just bring your item with its original product packaging and tags (if available), as well as a gift receipt or [proof of purchase](https://www.rei.com/help/returns#faq-0), to the customer service desk at any of our [REI locations](https://www.rei.com/map/store) for return or exchange.

**To return a gift by mail**, follow the [instructions for working directly with a carrier](https://www.rei.com/help/returns#faq-5) to ship a return, and include the following in your package:

1.  A note requesting a "gift recipient refund," along with the following information:

*   Name of gift recipient to be refunded
*   Gift recipient member number (if applicable)
*   Gift recipient mailing address
*   Gift recipient phone number
*   Gift recipient email address
*   **IMPORTANT:** If the gift recipient contact details are incomplete and/or a gift recipient refund is not clearly requested, the original purchaser will be refunded.

3.  A gift receipt or the original [proof of purchase](https://www.rei.com/help/returns#faq-0).

**Please note:** All gifts returned by mail will be refunded in the form of a check issued to the gift recipient name and address provided. We’ll email you once we’ve issued a check. Refunds by check can take up to 3 weeks, starting the day we receive your return.

The refund amount may differ from the original total paid due to tax rate differences between purchase and return locations.
