If you and your pet aren’t 100% satisfied, no worries. Return items within 365 days of purchase with free return shipping. Unless we inform you that a return is not required, you must return your purchase to be eligible for a refund or replacement.

We may decline a return or refund in cases of abuse, in our sole discretion.

Here’s how to [start a return](https://www.chewy.com/customer-care/returns/making-a-return/start-a-return).

### Items you can return include

*   Opened items like pet food and litter
*   Damaged or expired items
*   Personalized items
*   Vet Diet items

Chewy gift cards aren’t eligible for return once activated.

Chewy doesn’t take ownership of refunded items or returned items until the item arrives back to us. If your item gets lost on its way back, [chat live](https://www.chewy.com/customer-care/orders-payments-and-promotions/returns/return-policy/chewy-return-policy?signInFromChat=true) or call us at 1-800-672-4399 and we’ll be happy to help.

You may choose to receive your refund to your original payment method or as credit on your Chewy account balance. If we can’t use your original payment method (for example, due to an expired card), we will apply the refund to your Chewy account balance.

All items must be packed in a box before you send them back. You can reuse a Chewy box or another sturdy shipping box.

### Prescription items

To return prescription items, please contact us for more details. [Chat live](https://www.chewy.com/csrb/nma?pageContext=RX) or call us at 1-[877-977-3879](tel:877-977-3879).

### Refunding shipping costs

If you return your entire order for a refund, any shipping fees you paid will also be refunded. If only some items from your order are returned, shipping charges will not be refunded.

### Returning items purchased with a Chewy gift card without a Chewy account

If you do not have an account with Chewy and you make a purchase with a Chewy gift card through guest checkout or otherwise, you won’t be eligible for a refund. [See full gift card terms and conditions](https://www.chewy.com/app/content/gift-cards-terms?_ga=2.67851732.497126563.1598966686-172553841.1562937319) for more information.

If your refund has been approved and you don't need to return the item, you may be able to donate it instead of throwing it away.

Only donate items that are safe for another pet to use.

## Donate items that are safe to use

You can donate an item if it's:

*   Unopened
    
*   In good, safe, and usable condition
    

Consider donating eligible items to a local animal shelter or rescue organization.

## Don't donate unsafe or restricted items

Don't donate items that are:

*   Damaged or defective
    
*   Expired
    
*   Opened in a way that makes them unsafe to use
    
*   Otherwise unsuitable for another pet
    

Follow any product-specific disposal instructions before discarding these items.

### Veterinary diet items

Don't donate veterinary diet items. If you don't need to return one, safely discard any remaining product.

### Prescription medications

Never donate or share prescription medications.

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) recommends using a drug take-back location or mail-back program whenever possible. If those options aren't available, follow the disposal instructions on the medication label or the [FDA's guidance for disposing of unused medicines](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/where-and-how-dispose-unused-medicines).
