# Returns and Exchanges | Big 5 Sporting Goods

*   [home](https://www.big5sportinggoods.com/store/) >
*   returns & exchanges

If you are not satisfied with your purchase, simply return it in its original condition, along with the original receipt, to any Big 5 Sporting Goods store for an exchange or refund. Our general Return Policy does not apply to all items. For a list of exceptions and instructions on returning or exchanging specialty items, please read our [Return Exception Policy](https://www.big5sportinggoods.com/store/customer%20service/Return+Exception+Policy). These include: baseball bats, firearms, and air mattresses.

### Returns with a Receipt

*   Valid photo identification is requested for all returns and exchanges.
*   A refund will be issued in the same tender as the original purchase. If returning to a credit card, please have the card available.

### Returns without a Receipt

*   Valid photo identification is required for all returns and exchanges without a receipt.
*   Returns without a receipt will receive store credit.
*   The amount of credit issued will be at the last sale price or marked down price.

### Returns involving a Gift Receipt, Store Credit, Ticket to Shop or Merchandise Voucher

Store credit, but not a refund, may be issued for all returns involving a gift receipt, store credit, Ticket to Shop, merchandise voucher or certain other scrip.

### Returns of Online Purchases

*   Items purchased online from Big 5 Sporting Goods may be returned to any Big 5 Sporting Goods store with the original packing slip.
*   Credit will be returned to the same credit card used to make the online purchase. Please have the card available in-store at the time of return.

### Please Keep Your Receipt!

If we fail to offer you a receipt, or if the total does not match the amount you paid, please ask to see the Store Manager before you leave.

To the extent permitted by law, information from your photo ID may be recorded at the time of return and retained in a company-wide database of customer return activity that is used in connection with authorizing returns.

### Acceptable Identification for all Transactions Include a Current/Valid U.S.:

*   Picture Driver's License
*   Picture State ID Card
*   Active Duty Military ID
*   Passport (only for Traveler's Checks)
