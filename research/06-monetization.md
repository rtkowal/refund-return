## Reality check (brief item 6)
- **Confirmed:** a "File your return with {Brand}" click is not monetizable. Brand affiliate programs pay on new sales, not returns. The /go/ layer should still track it, because the data proves intent to partners.
- **Monetizable exits, in rank order:**
  1. Flight claims on EU/UK-departing flights. A real per-claim payout, but **US domestic flights have no EU261-style compensation**, so US-only flights earn nothing here.
  2. Subscription-cancellation fintechs: Rocket Money and Privacy.com both own cancel-SERPs. Program terms not yet researched.
  3. "Buy the replacement" offers on return pages, through Amazon Associates, Impact or CJ.
  4. Display ads: Journey is reachable early.
  5. Seller-generator SaaS referrals: weaker than the brief assumed.
