# What Is Nike's Return Policy?

We stand behind our shoes and gear. If it’s been less than 60 days since your purchase, simply [return](https://www.nike.com/help/a/how-to-return) the item. If it’s been more than 60 days and your item is potentially defective or flawed, please see our [warranty information](https://www.nike.com/help/a/shoe-warranty) for additional details.

If you have a Nike x LEGO brick set with missing or damaged parts, you can [request replacement pieces directly from the LEGO website](https://www.nike.com/help/a/lego).

If you have a potentially defective or flawed Hyperice item, please [contact Hyperice directly](mailto:customersupport@hyperice.com) (even if it’s within 60 days of purchase).
