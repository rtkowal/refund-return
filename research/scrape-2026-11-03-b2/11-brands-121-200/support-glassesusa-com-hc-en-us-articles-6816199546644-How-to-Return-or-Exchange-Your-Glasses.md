# How to Return or Exchange Your Glasses – GlassesUSA.com Help Center & FAQs for Glasses and Contacts

Return or exchange your order for FREE within 60 days of delivery using our self-service portal or Live Chat.

## 4 Simple Steps to Return or Exchange

### Step 1: Start Your Request

*   **Via Chat:** Open Live Chat and type "Start a return".
    
*   **Via Portal:** Go to our [Returns Portal](https://www.glassesusa.com/rma) and enter your **Order ID** and **Email Address**. Select Exchange, Store Credit, or Refund.
    

### Step 2: Get Your Free Label or QR Code

*   **US Orders:** Return shipping is **100% FREE**. You will instantly receive a prepaid USPS shipping label or a digital Happy Returns QR Code via email.
*   **International Orders:** Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility.

### Step 3: Package Your Items

*   **Eyeglasses:** Place your glasses inside their protective case. **Lost your case? No problem!** Simply wrap your glasses carefully in bubble wrap or soft paper so they don't scratch in transit. Custom prescription lenses are 100% covered!
*   **Contact Lenses**: Boxes must be completely unopened, undamaged, and factory-sealed.

### Step 4: Drop Off Your Package

*   **Option A (Fastest - No Box Needed):** Take your items and digital QR code to any local Happy Returns Bar.
*   **Option B (USPS):** Attach the printed prepaid label to your box and drop it off at any USPS post office or collection box.

## Processing Timelines & Refunds

Once your return is scanned at Happy Returns or received at our warehouse, processing takes approximately 4 business days:

*   **Exchanges:** Your new exchange order enters lab production immediately after processing**.**
    
*   **Store Credit:** Issued instantly via email after processing.
    
*   **Original Payment Refunds:** Processed within 10 days. Please allow an additional3 to 5 business days for your bank to post the funds.
