# What is your return policy?

Answer

## Returns & Refunds

Everything you need to know about returning your Skechers online purchase

**Guest Orders** Return within 45 days of shipment

**Skechers Plus Members** Return within 90 days of shipment

**Return Condition** Items must be unworn.  
Preorders not eligible for return.

Return by Mail

To start a return, [log in](https://www.skechers.com/login/?rurl=1) to your Skechers Plus account and find your order in your purchase history. If you checked out as a guest, visit the [Orders and Returns](https://www.skechers.com/on/demandware.store/Sites-USSkechers-Site/en_US/Order-Track?action=startReturn) page to get started.

**Return Requirements:**

*   Please return unworn items in their original packaging.

**Return Label Options:**

*   If you selected a QR code, show it to a representative at a [UPS service center](https://www.theupsstore.com/tools/find-a-store). UPS will print the return label for you.
*   If you prefer to print your own label, select "Print My Label" and attach it to the package.

Returning more than one package by mail? [Click here for instructions](https://customercare.skechers.com/HelpCenter/s/article/How-do-I-return-more-than-one-package-by-mail).

**Please note:**

*   QR Code and prepaid labels are not available for [APO/FPO locations or US Territories](https://customercare.skechers.com/HelpCenter/s/article/Do-you-ship-to-APO-FPO-US-Territories).
*   We'll send you a confirmation as soon as your return has been processed. Please allow 10 business days once the return is received.
*   Free return postage applies only to orders within the return period.
*   Initial shipping costs are non-refundable.
*   If we are unable to refund the original form of payment, an E-gift card will be issued via email.
*   Pre-order items are final sale and are not eligible for return or exchange.

Return In-Store

Return your unworn items at any [US Skechers store](https://www.skechers.com/store-locator.html) in the US (excluding Guam). You may download your receipt through your account or by visiting the [Orders and Returns](https://www.skechers.com/on/demandware.store/Sites-USSkechers-Site/en_US/Order-Track?action=startReturn) page.

*   **In-store pick-up orders:** Please return to any Skechers store near you.
*   **Cash App Afterpay and Klarna:** Orders paid with Cash App Afterpay or Klarna will be issued an exchange or merchandise credit if returned to a store location. To receive a refund to your original form of payment, please return your order by mail.
*   **Pre-order items:** Final sale, not eligible for return or exchange.

Important Return Conditions

Please note that if a product is returned outside of the return period, or if the item is worn, damaged, or not in its original packaging, Skechers will be unable to honor a refund for the merchandise. Pre-order items are final sale and are not eligible for return or exchange.

We only accept returns for purchases made on Skechers.com.
