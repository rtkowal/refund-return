**Extended Holiday Return Period**

For in-store and Sephora.com purchases of merchandise made on or after Friday, October 30, 2026 through and including Thursday, December 31, 2026, we offer an extended holiday return period as an exception to our normal Return Policy. So long as such merchandise is returned or exchanged on or before Sunday, January 31, 2027, we will allow in-store exchanges, store credit in the form of a Sephora merchandise credit for returns made in-store, or online credit for returns of Sephora.com purchase_s_ made by mail initiated on Sephora.com. Approved returns within 30 days of purchase with a receipt are refunded to the original form of payment (excluding PayPal online purchases). Merchandise credits can only be used at Sephora stores and not on Sephora.com. Online credits can be used at Sephora.com but are not valid for use in Sephora stores. Any purchases made after Friday, January 1, 2027 will follow our normal Return Policy guidelines. All other terms of our Return Policy continue to apply including for merchandise returned within 30 days of purchase.

Only purchases made at Sephora and Sephora.com are included in the extended Holiday Return policies.

## Returns Policy

If you are not completely satisfied with a Sephora purchase or gift for any reason, Sephora welcomes you to return new or gently used products for a full refund to your original method of payment if returned within 30 days of purchase, in most cases. In order to complete the return, you must have a proof of purchase.

Please review our return, refund, and exchange policies below for purchases made on Sephora.com, purchases made in Sephora stores, purchases made on TikTok Shop, and purchases made on third party on-demand delivery marketplaces such as Instacart, Doordash, and Uber Eats. Products must be returned in new or gently used condition. Sephora monitors return activity for abuse and reserves the right to limit returns or exchanges at Sephora in all instances. Beauty Insider points and spend associated with all refunds will be removed when the transaction is processed. All returns are subject to validation and approval at Sephora’s discretion. If a return is not approved by Sephora for any reason, the item may not be returned to you. We may ask you for a driver’s license or government ID to verify your identity.

## Sephora.com Purchases

Sephora.com purchases can be easily returned for FREE (no return shipping or handling fees) to any of our [Sephora locations](https://www.sephora.com/happening/stores/sephora-near-me?icid2=footer_help_storelocations_link_external) for fast processing or by mail if you use our pre-paid return shipping label (follow the return procedures described below). Purchases made on Sephora.com and in Sephora stores cannot be returned or exchanged at Sephora at Kohl’s. We regret that we are unable to refund your original shipping charges, if applicable, unless the return was a result of our error. Please allow 30 days for your return to be processed if sent by mail. You will receive an email confirmation once your return has been processed. Your financial institution may take longer to reflect the transaction.

## Online Purchase Return Procedures

Here are two easy ways to return your item from a Sephora.com or a third-party marketplace order\*:

### 1\. Sephora.com Returns by Mail

▸ [Click here to start or track a return](https://returns.narvar.com/sephora/returns?locale=en_US)

♦ Using the link above indicate which items will be returned and the reason for each return.\*\* ♦ No packing slip is needed if the Prepaid Return Label is used. ♦ Package your return item in the manufacturer's product box and Sephora packaging (if available). Otherwise, pack your return in a well-padded envelope or box to prevent damage in transit. ♦ Print the Prepaid Return Label or request a printerless FedEx Mobile Return Code (if available) from the link above. ♦ Drop off the package at one of the specified FedEx locations.\*\*\* ♦ Track your return using the link above. ♦ You will receive an email confirmation when your return is processed.

It takes up to 30 days to process returns and credit your account or to issue an online store credit. Please note that your financial institution may take longer to reflect the transaction.

\* Items purchased at Sephora stores, Buy Online Pickup in Store purchases and Sephora items ordered on Instacart, Doordash, or Uber Eats cannot be returned by mail.

\*\* Gift Cards, certain Sephora Favorites Kits and some intimate care items are final sale and cannot be returned by mail or in store.

\*\*\* Items with specific shipping restrictions (hazmat items) are not eligible for printerless returns and must be returned via USPS. Click [here](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/shipping-information#hazmat) for more information on hazmat items.

\*\*\*\* Items purchased on Sephora TikTok Shop must be registered for return on TikTok Shop and returned following TikTok Shop instructions. TikTok Shop returns will not be accepted at Sephora stores or on sephora.com.

### 2\. Sephora.com Returns to Store Location

Visit any free standing [Sephora store](https://www.sephora.com/happening/stores/sephora-near-me?icid2=footer_help_storelocations_link_external) to return an item purchased from Sephora.com or third-party marketplace within 30 days of purchase. Please bring any of the following: an original packing slip, Sephora.com Order or Shipping Confirmation email, the Sephora.com order on your mobile device, your Beauty Insider information. Additionally, please bring the debit or credit card you used to place your order. Returns made within 30 days will be refunded to the credit or debit card of your choice. Purchases made on Sephora.com and in Sephora stores cannot be returned or exchanged at Sephora at Kohl’s.

## Gift Returns

If you are returning a Sephora gift that you received by mail and do not want the refund to go to the purchaser, please visit your local Sephora store for assistance with an exchange or a merchandise credit. Please bring any of the following: an original packing slip, Sephora.com Order or Shipping Confirmation email, or Gift Receipt email. Do not make a return by mail.

## Sephora at Kohl’s Returns

**THIS POLICY IS EFFECTIVE FOR ALL PURCHASES MADE ON AND AFTER APRIL 24, 2025.**

Merchandise purchased at Sephora at Kohl’s locations or on kohls.com cannot be returned to Sephora locations or mailed to Sephora. To return an item purchased at Sephora at Kohl’s or ordered on kohls.com, please visit a Kohl’s retail store or follow the Kohl’s [return guidelines](https://www.kohls.com/faq/article/893).

Sephora at Kohl’s merchandise must be returned within 30 days from date of purchase for a refund to the original form of payment. Product should be in new or “gently used” condition. Non-receipted returns are not allowed.

Customers returning merchandise purchased using a Sephora Gift Card or PayPal will be issued a Kohl’s merchandise credit, which can be used in Kohl’s stores and at kohls.com.

Merchandise purchased at Sephora locations or on sephora.com cannot be returned to Sephora at Kohl’s locations or mailed to Kohl’s. To return an item purchased at Sephora please follow the Sephora return guidelines.
