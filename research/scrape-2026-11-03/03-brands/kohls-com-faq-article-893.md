# Kohl's Return Policy

## Kohl’s 2026 Holiday Extended Return Policy

The holidays start early at Kohl's! Beginning October 4, you can shop for gifts with confidence knowing eligible purchases are covered by our extended holiday return policy, giving friends and family more time after the holidays to return or exchange their gifts.

**Kohl’s Merchandise (Excluding Sephora)**

*   Purchases made **October 4, 2026 – December 30, 2026** are eligible for return through **January 31, 2027**.
*   This extension applies to all Kohl’s merchandise, including Premium Electronics and Watches, Jewelry, and Mattresses.
*   **Sephora at Kohl’s products are not included** in this extended return policy and follow their standard return guidelines shown below.

**Sephora at Kohl’s**

*   Purchases made at **Sephora at Kohl’s** between **October 30, 2026 – December 30, 2026** are eligible for return through **January 31, 2027**.

**Regular Return Policy Still Applies Outside the Holiday Window**

*   Items purchased before **October 4, 2026** or after **December 30, 2026** follow our standard return policy:
    *   Most Kohl’s merchandise can be returned up to **90 days** from the date of purchase.
    *   Kohl’s Cardholders enjoy **up to 120 days** to return most items.
    *   Premium Electronics and Watches normally have a **30-day** return window.
    *   Sephora at Kohl’s products follow the Sephora standard 30-day return policy.

**How to Return**

*   Returns can be made in-store at any Kohl’s location or by mail (if applicable).
*   Please bring your original receipt, Kohl’s Rewards account information, or the credit card used at purchase.
*   Items must be in original condition and packaging (when applicable).

## Standard Return Policy

Kohl’s gladly accepts most returns with or without a receipt.

*   **Items purchased with a Kohl’s Card**: You have within **120-days** from purchase to make a return.
    
*   **All other payment types**: You have within **90-days** from purchase to make a return.
    
*   **Returning Items Purchased Using EBT**: All items purchased with an EBT card will follow Kohl’s Standard Return Policy. If you return an item originally purchased with an EBT card, your refund will be in the form of KMC (Kohl’s Merchandise Credit).
    

Some items have a shorter return window:

*   **Premium Electronics and Watches**: May be returned within **30 days** of purchase with the original packaging and a valid receipt or account lookup.
    
*   **Sephora at Kohl’s Purchases**: Must be new or gently used and returned within **30 days** of purchase, and accompanied by a valid receipt or account lookup.
    
*   **Mattresses** must be returned within **30 days** of purchase, and accompanied by a valid receipt or account lookup.
    
*   **Beverages**: Non-refundable and cannot be exchanged.
    
*   Opened or Unsealed **Breast Pumps and Accessories**: Non-refundable and cannot be exchanged.
    
*   **Trading cards** are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged.
    
*   **Blind box items/toys** are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged.
    
*   **Baby Registry Items**: May be returned **up to 365 days** from purchase with a gift receipt or original receipt. View the complete Baby Registry return policy.
    

The return window begins at different times depending on how the purchase was fulfilled:

*   For in-store purchases, the return window begins **on the purchase date.**
*   For shipped online orders, the return window begins **on the ship date.**
*   For Buy Online, Pick Up in Store (BOPUS) and Buy Online, Ship to Store (BOSS) orders, the return window begins **on the date the order is ready for pickup.**

Verification Notice: Returns may be subject to third-party verification and system approval. Your ID information and return history may be collected where permitted by law. Kohl’s reserves the right to limit or refuse refunds.

## How to Make a Return

### Free In-Store Returns

Returning in-store is the fastest and easiest option. Simply:

1.  Bring your item(s) to any Kohl’s store.
2.  Visit the Customer Service counter for assistance.
3.  Your refund should appear on your credit card within about seven days, but it may take longer depending on your bank’s processing time. For details, please check with your bank.

Most returns are accepted with or without a receipt. Associates can look up your order using:

*   Order number
*   Kohl’s Rewards account
*   Credit card number
*   Return barcode in the Kohl’s App

**No Receipt?**

Most returns are accepted with or without a receipt. Associates can look up your order using:

*   Order number
*   Kohl’s Rewards account
*   Credit card number
*   Return barcode in the Kohl’s App

If you don’t have a receipt and we are unable to look up your purchase, you’ll receive a Kohl’s Merchandise Credit for the lowest discounted 13-week sale price. A valid photo ID may be required.

**Items purchased in a store can only be returned at a store—** you can’t return these items via mail.

Items delivered via our Threshold Delivery Service can’t be returned in store. If you need to return one of these items, follow the Threshold Return instructions.

### Returns by Mail

You can return eligible online Kohl’s purchases by mail. Items purchased in a store may only be returned to the store. Please note:

*   Customers are responsible for postage.
*   Kohl’s does not provide prepaid return labels.
*   Shipping costs and surcharges are non-refundable.
*   It may take up to 7-10 days after we receive your return to process and issue your refund.
*   Refunds will be issued to the card used to make your purchase. Merchandise credits will be mailed to the billing address on file, unless a different address is provided.
*   Exchanges are not available by mail — please visit a store for size or color exchanges.

**How to return by mail:**

*   [Sign into your account](https://www.kohls.com/myaccount/dashboard) and navigate to “Order History.”
*   From the Order Details page, select the order you would like to return.
*   Next choose “Start a Return" and select the items you wish to return.
*   You’ll receive a packing slip and instructions on how to ship back the item(s).

Not signed in? You can also start a return by simply entering your Order Number and Email via our [Returns Portal here](https://returns.narvar.com/kohls/returns?locale=en_US).

### Threshold Returns

Larger items that required [freighted delivery](https://www.kohls.com/faq/article/1161) need to be returned via a delivery service and cannot be accepted in a store.

**Fees and Refunds**:

*   Shipping costs and surcharges are non-refundable.
*   There is a 15% restocking fee unless the returned item is defective.

**How to start a Threshold Return**:

1.  Follow the instructions printed near the bottom of your order invoice, marked with a stop sign.
2.  Call Kohl’s Customer Service at 844-564-5701 to arrange your return.

## Kohl’s Cash® and Returns

Kohl’s Cash® is typically awarded in $10 increments for every $50 spent on qualifying merchandise. Your refund amount may be adjusted depending on whether Kohl’s Cash® was earned or redeemed for the items being returned.

### Returning Items That Earned Kohl’s Cash® that Was Never Redeemed

If you return items from a purchase that earned Kohl’s Cash®, your Kohl’s Cash® may be reduced or voided if the return lowers your total purchase below the amount needed to earn it.

When your return is processed, we recalculate your purchase total. If that total no longer meets the earning threshold, your Kohl’s Cash® will be adjusted accordingly.

**Example:** You spend $100 and earn $20 in Kohl’s Cash®. Later, you return a $60 item. Your adjusted total becomes $40, which no longer qualifies for Kohl’s Cash®. Since you haven’t used the $20 Kohl’s Cash® yet, it will be voided.

### Returning Items That Earned and Redeemed Kohl’s Cash®

If you’ve already used your Kohl’s Cash® and then return items from the purchase that earned it, your refund will be reduced by the amount of Kohl’s Cash® that is no longer earned.

**Example:** You spend $100 and earn $20 in Kohl’s Cash®. You use that Kohl’s Cash® on a later purchase. Then, you return $50 worth of items from your original $100 order.

Your new total is $50, which only qualifies for $10 in Kohl’s Cash®. Because you already used $20, your refund will be reduced by $10, which is the unearned portion.

### If You Return Items Purchased Using Kohl’s Cash®

If you return an item you bought using Kohl’s Cash®, don’t worry—you’ll get all or a portion of your Kohl’s Cash® back.

*   If your Kohl’s Cash® is still valid, the amount will be added back to that same Kohl’s Cash® coupon.
*   If it has expired, you’ll receive a new Kohl’s Cash® coupon for the same amount.

You earned that Kohl’s Cash®, and it will not be taken away from you when returning those items.

**Example:** You use $20 in Kohl’s Cash® to buy a sweater, then decide to return it. If your Kohl’s Cash® is still valid, $20 will be credited back to your existing coupon. If it’s expired, we’ll issue you a new $20 Kohl’s Cash® coupon to use on a future purchase.

## In-Store Exchanges

Most items are eligible for in-store exchanges within the applicable return window. Exchanges by mail are not available.

Exchanges must be for the same item (brand, style, and ticketed price) — only size or color may differ. If the replacement item has a different ticketed price, an even exchange cannot be completed.

In some situations you cannot exchange one item for another:

*   Opened or unsealed breast pumps and pumping accessories.
*   Items exclusively sold online or Marketplace items.

### To make an exchange in store:

1.  Bring your item to any Kohl’s store.
2.  The store must have your replacement item in stock to complete an exchange. We can’t guarantee that your replacement item will be available.
3.  Visit the Customer Service counter to start your exchange.
4.  If an exchange can’t be completed, you can still return your item for a refund or merchandise credit (as applicable).

**Tip**: Even if you don't have your receipt, we can still look up your purchase using:

*   Order number
*   Kohl’s Rewards account
*   Credit card number
*   Return barcode in the Kohl’s App

### Gift Exchanges

Items can be exchanged with a gift receipt within the applicable return window. If you don’t have a gift receipt, you’ll receive a Kohl’s Merchandise Credit for the lowest discounted 13-week sale price. A valid photo ID may be required.

## Sephora at Kohl's Returns

We only accept returns for items purchased through [Kohls.com](http://kohls.com/), the Kohl’s app or at a Sephora at Kohl’s store. Items must be new or gently used and accompanied by a receipt.

*   Returns must be made **within 30 days** of purchase.
    
    *   **Extended Holiday Return Period**
        
        Sephora at Kohl’s purchases made October 30, 2026, through December 30, 2026, are eligible for return through January 30, 2027.
        
*   Returns without a receipt are not accepted.
    
*   Final sale Sephora products cannot be returned.
    

**Where to Return Sephora Purchases**

*   Sephora items purchased at Sephora at Kohl’s or on [Kohls.com](https://www.kohls.com/) may be returned at any Kohl’s store, even if that location does not have a Sephora at Kohl’s.
*   Sephora items purchased on [Kohls.com](https://www.kohls.com/) or at a Sephora at Kohl’s location cannot be returned to standalone Sephora stores or mailed to Sephora.
*   Items purchased directly from Sephora.com or standalone Sephora stores cannot be returned to Kohl’s or Sephora at Kohl’s locations. Please follow Sephora’s standard return process for those purchases.

## Baby Registry Returns

Kohl’s offers an extended return period of up to 365 days for verified, eligible purchases from open registries.

*   Applies to items connected to a verified registry at the time of purchase. A verified registry purchase means the item was bought online directly from the registry or in-store by scanning the registry’s QR code at checkout. When shopping online, verified items will show a “Baby Registry” label in your cart.
*   The 365-day window begins on the purchase date.
*   Exceptions: Opened or unsealed breast pumps and select accessories are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged.

### Proof of Purchase Requirements

**Gift Receivers:**

*   Must provide an original receipt, gift receipt or the item must be a verified purchase off your registry to qualify for the extended return period.
*   To confirm if an item is a verified Kohl’s purchase, visit the Gift Tracker tab in the owner’s view within your registry and check the individual item.
*   Refunds will be issued as Kohl’s Merchandise Credit (KMC).
*   For items not linked to your registry at checkout or without a receipt, you’ll receive a Kohl’s Merchandise Credit for the lowest discounted 13-week sale price. A valid photo ID may be required.

**Gift Purchasers:**

*   Must provide an original receipt, gift receipt or successful lookup to receive a refund or exchange. Associates can use your order number, Kohl’s Rewards account information, credit card number or return barcode within the app to look up your order.
*   Make sure to purchase items directly from the registry if shopping online or to have an associate scan the registry QR code while checking out if shopping in-store. This will allow for easier returns for your gift recipients.

## Return Amazon Orders at Kohl’s

Amazon returns are easy and free at most Kohl’s stores (excluding Anchorage, Alaska).

**How to Return Amazon Items**

1.  Begin your return in [Amazon’s Online Return Center](https://www.amazon.com/).
2.  Select the Kohl’s Dropoff option.
3.  Amazon will email and display a QR code in your account.
4.  Bring your item(s) and QR code to a participating Kohl’s store.
5.  A Kohl’s associate will pack, label, and ship your return for free.
