# 07 — B2B angle

**Benchmark data (cite, don't scrape):**
- NRF + Happy Returns 2025 Retail Returns Landscape (published 2025-10-15) gives these figures:
  - **$849.9B** returned in 2025, down from $890B in 2024
  - **15.8%** return rate by sales value, down from 16.9%
  - **19.3%** online return rate
  - **17%** holiday return rate
  - **9%** of returns fraudulent
- Source: [NRF press release](https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/consumers-expected-to-return-nearly-850-billion-in-merchandise-in-2025).
- The rates are by sales value, not order count. Say so wherever we cite them.

**Seller return-policy generator:**
- Demand is small: "return policy generator" 200/mo (KD 54, CPC $1.73), "shopify return policy" 200/mo (KD 36).
- The affiliate case is weak. Loop has no commission program. ReturnGO's reported 10% recurring is unverified. Redo and AfterShip programs weren't found (see 06).
- Value: it's a credibility and link asset, plus Google `MerchantReturnPolicy` structured-data output (schema.org and Google merchant-listing docs are in batch 1). That output is a real differentiator over Shopify's free generator.
- **Verdict: build it, Shopify and Amazon first. Expect low revenue.**

**Owner input needed:** first-hand Shopify-merchant insight. What return-app pricing or partner deals have you actually been offered? Do returns apps pay referral fees to merchants or agencies?

**Data licensing (policy DB to returns-tech, cashback and comparison sites):** only credible once 200+ brands are kept fresh, so 6+ months after launch. Park it until then.

**Fraud and abuse content** (wardrobing, the 9% fraud stat) is linkable B2B content. Low priority.
