# Returning A GNC Item to a Physical Store

GNC guarantees satisfaction. If a customer is not pleased with any product we sell, we will offer a refund or exchange. All products are refundable/exchangeable within 30 days from the date of your order from GNC.com or payment made in a GNC Retail store with a valid sales receipt or GNC.com packing slip. Refunds/Exchanges must be processed when the customer presents the goods regardless of the GNC store where the product was originally purchased. Please note that Bill Me Later® purchases cannot be returned to a local GNC store. Please refer to your packing slip for return instructions.

**POLICY**

In order to better serve our customers, a Cash Refund and Exchange Refund policy has been established. Customers can return their purchase with a valid sales receipt and a valid photo ID within 30 days from the date of purchase.

**Cash Refunds:**Cash refunds are given on product purchased with cash and brought back, used or unused, within 30 days of the purchase date with a valid sales receipt and a valid photo ID within 30 days from the date of purchase.

**Exchanges:**If the customer returns non-opened, used or opened product, an exchange will be offered based on the amount paid (as shown on the sales receipt or GNC.com packing slip).

In the absence of a sales receipt or GNC.com packing slip, an exchange will be offered on any GNC brand product based on the lowest sale price or Member Price within the last 60 days.

Exchanges will be offered on GNC brand products within 30 days from the purchase date with or without a sales receipt.

No exchanges or refunds will be given on non-GNC brand items unless they are accompanied with a valid sales receipt.

_Note: The exchanged product is to be the same retail price as that of the returned product. If the exchanged item is a higher price than the returned item, the customer will then be required to pay the balance due._

**Merchandise Credit (GNC Gift Card):**If the customer returns an unused, unopened, used or opened GNC product without a receipt and does not wish to process an exchange for a product, a GNC Gift Card will be issued. The GNC Gift Card will be issued for the product value as outlined in the Exchange policy. A return where the original payment was a GNC Gift Card will be issued another GNC Gift Card as a Merchandise Credit.

**Credit Card:**Credits will be issued to the original charge account when the product is returned within 30 days from the purchase date with a valid sales receipt or GNC.com packing slip. Cash refunds will not be given. In the case of a lost, stolen, or closed credit card, the customer must provide a letter (on letterhead) from their credit card company that states the account was closed in good faith. The letter and valid sales receipt or GNC.com packing slip must be presented at the store you are returning the products.

**Check Refunds:**If you paid by check, with a valid sales receipt, we will offer a cash refund ten business days after your purchase.

**Debit Card:**Debit card refunds will be issued as a cash refund within 30 days from the date of purchase with a valid sales receipt. Debit card refunds may also be issued to the original charged account if there is not sufficient cash available.

**GNC MEMBERSHIP REFUND**

GNC Memberships are non-refundable.
