# Cancel Adobe trial or subscription

Last updated on Jul 24, 2026

Learn how to cancel your Adobe trial or subscription through your Adobe account.

You can cancel your subscription directly from your Adobe account if you purchased it from Adobe. For subscriptions purchased through third-party providers such as [Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428), [Google](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&oco=1#zippy=%2Cwhat-happens-after-you-cancel), or [Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/help/4522164/microsoft-account-how-to-cancel-your-subscription?fref=services_help_cancelSub), follow their specific cancellation process.

In the Plans and payment window, select Manage plan for the plan you want to cancel.

   ![The Plans window, where the Manage plan button is highlighted under the currently active plan.](https://helpx-prod.scene7.com/is/image/HelpxProdLoc/manage-plans-not-india?$pjpeg$&jpegSize=300&wid=1600)

You can also check all the apps and services included in your plan.

Select Cancel your plan.

   ![The Manage Plan window, with the Cancel your plan button highlighted in the End your service section under Available actions.](https://helpx-prod.scene7.com/is/image/HelpxProdLoc/cancel-plan-135?$pjpeg$&jpegSize=300&wid=1920)

Manage your subscription by changing, upgrading, or cancelling your plan at any time.

Check your plan details and then select Continue to cancel.

Select the reason for cancellation, and then select Continue.

Review the cancellation details and then select Confirm cancellation.

If you have more than one plan, each plan must be cancelled separately. Cancelling one plan does not cancel your other active plans. Check your [Adobe account](https://account.adobe.com/plans) to review which plans are still active.  

## What happens after you cancel

Your access to paid apps and services continues until the end of your current billing period. Your Adobe account stays active, and you keep access to [free membership benefits](https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/help/benefits-creative-cloud-membership.html#DoIgetafreeCreativeCloudmembershipevenifIhavecanceledasubscription). Learn more about [account access after plan cancellation](https://helpx.adobe.com/account/individual/subscriptions-and-plans/renewals-and-cancellations/account-access-after-plan-cancellation.html).

### Reactivate within billing period

If you change your mind after cancellation and you're within the billing period, you can easily reactivate your plan from your [Adobe account](https://account.adobe.com/plans). Once you reactivate, your apps, services, and storage will be restored, and billing will continue as scheduled.

## Cancellation fee and refunds

A refund depends on when you cancel and the type of subscription you purchased. For most plans, you get a full refund if you cancel within 14 days of your initial purchase. Learn more about [refund eligibility when you cancel your plan](https://helpx.adobe.com/account/individual/terms-policies-and-regulations/adobe-subscription-terms.html).
