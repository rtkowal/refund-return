# Hollister Co.

**In Stores:** Bring your merchandise in its original condition and your shipping confirmation email or invoice, and we’ll honor the original price paid.

**Online:** Start your exchange [here](https://www.hollisterco.com/shop/us/help/startmyreturn). Enter your order number and email address, then select your new size or color, and drop your package in the mail.

**In Stores:** Bring your merchandise in its original condition and your shipping confirmation email or invoice. Refunds are credited to the original form of payment.

**Online:** Look for the return label in your package, then drop it off at the post office, and you’ll receive an email confirmation of your receipt. A $7 fee will be applied to your return.
