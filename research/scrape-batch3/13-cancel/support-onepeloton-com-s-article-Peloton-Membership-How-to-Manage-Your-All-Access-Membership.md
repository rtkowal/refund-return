# Peloton Membership: How to Manage Your All-Access Membership

## **Overview**

A Peloton All-Access Membership is the Membership required to access content on a your Peloton Bike, Bike+, Tread, Tread+, or Row. With your Peloton All-Access Membership, you’ll get the most value-packed Peloton Membership for the whole household. 

**Note:** Members with an all access membership \[AAM\]  can use their membership on other Commercial bikes.

## **Finding Your Membership**

If you are unsure of your Membership details, take the following steps to locate your Membership:

1.  Go to [account.onepeloton.com/mymembership.](http://onepeloton.com/mymembership)
    
2.  Your active Membership will be shown under the “Memberships” section.
    

* * *

## **Membership Billing and Payments**

All Peloton All-Access Memberships recur on a monthly basis until they are canceled, in accordance with Peloton's [Membership Terms](https://www.onepeloton.com/membership-terms). All Peloton App Memberships recur on either a monthly or annual basis depending on your current membership.

You will not be billed for your All-Access  Membership until you receive and activate your Membership on your Peloton touchscreen. Once activated, the credit card on file will be charged each month on a recurring basis until cancelled or otherwise deactivated. App memberships will be billed upon purchase or after the end of any free-trial period. 

For uninterrupted service, please ensure that the credit card on your account is up to date and valid. We can only accept payments from a credit, debit, or prepaid card that is associated with a U.S. billing address. We also accept Peloton gift cards.

If you are looking for your Peloton order or Membership invoice, please navigate [here](https://support.onepeloton.com/s/article/Peloton-Account-How-to-Locate-Product-and-Membership-Invoices).

### **Updating or Changing Your Payment Method:**

Managing the payment method used for your Peloton Membership is easy. To change or update your payment information, please follow the steps below:

1.  Navigate to [account.onepeloton.com](http://account.onepeloton.com/) and log in to your account.
    
2.  Scroll down to Payment methods on file.
    
3.  Click on the pencil icon to edit your payment.

### **All-Access Membership Billing FAQs:**

**Q: Do I Need To Pay For Two Memberships If I Own Two Different Peloton Hardware Products?**

**A:** Our $49.99/month Peloton [All-Access Membership](https://www.onepeloton.com/membership) will allow you access to content across 1 Bike, 1 Tread, 1 Row, and 1 Guide per household.

Therefore, if you already have a Peloton Bike product and choose to purchase a Peloton Tread or Peloton Row, you can continue to pay $49.99/month for unlimited access to class content across all products.

Please note: All-Access Memberships can **only** be shared across products of different types (e.g. Bike, Bike+, Tread, Tread+, Row, Guide). **In the case of having 2 products of the same type (e.g. Bike/Bike+ OR Tread/Tread+) two separate All-Access Memberships are required.**

**Q: Can I change my billing date?**

**A:** No, your card will be charged on the same day each month as the day you first activated your All-Access Membership. For example, if you received your Peloton product and activated your Membership on the 16th, you will be billed every month on the 16th. If you activated your All-Access Membership on the 31st, you will be billed on the 1st of the month following a month with 30 or fewer days.

**Q: Is Peloton able to provide an invoice of my charges?**

**A:** You can find steps for generating Peloton purchase receipts or invoices [here](https://support.onepeloton.com/s/article/Peloton-Account-How-to-Locate-Product-and-Membership-Invoices). Members are able to generate invoices for a period of up to 18 months prior to the date of creation.

**Q: I noticed an error in my Membership billing statement. / I have been billed in error for a Membership. Can I get a refund?**

**A:** If you believe you have been billed in error for a Membership, please notify us within 60 days of the billing date by contacting Member Support by clicking [here](https://support.onepeloton.com/s/contact-us?language=en_US). Peloton will not issue refunds or credits after the expiration of this 60-day period, except where required by applicable law. For more information, please refer to our [Membership Terms](https://www.onepeloton.com/membership-terms).

## **Pausing Your All-Access Membership**

If you need a short break, we also offer the ability to pause your All-Access Membership for 1, 2 or 3 months. While your Membership is paused, you will be unable to access any Peloton content on your Peloton Bike, Tread, or Row, or the Peloton App. The pause period starts at the beginning of your next billing cycle and then billing automatically resumes at the end of the pause.

If you’d like to pause your All-Access membership, click [here,](https://www.onepeloton.com/mymembership) then click ‘Manage’ next to the subscription, then ‘Pause or cancel’ to make your selection.

You can unpause at any time by logging into your subscription on your Bike, Tread, or Row or via the website at [onepeloton.com/mymembership](https://www.onepeloton.com/mymembership). 

If you expect to be unable to use your Peloton All-Access Membership for longer than 3 months, we recommend cancelling your Membership and reactivating when you’re able to do so. 

* * *

## **Cancelling Your All-Access Membership**

If you expect to be unable to use your Peloton All-Access Membership for longer than 3 months due to travel, injury, illness, relocation, pregnancy, or any other reason, you can cancel your Membership. 

If you’d like to cancel your All-Access membership, click [here,](https://www.onepeloton.com/mymembership) then click ‘Manage’ next to the subscription, then ‘Pause or cancel’ to make your selection. You will continue to have access to Peloton content through the end of your current Membership billing period, at which point your All-Access Membership will cancel rather than renew. 

You can reactivate at any time by logging into your subscription on your Bike, Tread, or Row or via the website at [onepeloton.com/mymembership](https://www.onepeloton.com/mymembership).
