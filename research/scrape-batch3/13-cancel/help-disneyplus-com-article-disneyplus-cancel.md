# How to cancel Disney+ | Disney+ Help Center

Learn the steps to cancel your Disney+ subscription. Disney+ subscribers are able to cancel their subscription at any time, effective at the end of their current subscription term.

Please note that cancelling your Disney+ subscription will not delete your Disney+ account or the [MyDisney](https://help.disneyplus.com/article/disneyplus-login-credentials) account you use for other services and experiences across [The Walt Disney Family of Companies](https://my.disney.com/), if applicable.

## [](https://help.disneyplus.com/article/disneyplus-cancel#)**How do I cancel my Disney+, bundle, or add-on subscription?  
**

If you’re **billed directly by Disney+** and would like to cancel your subscription:

1.  Log in to [DisneyPlus.com](https://www.disneyplus.com/) through a computer or mobile browser
2.  Select your **Profile**
3.  Select **Account**
4.  Select your **Disney+ subscription** under **Subscription**
5.  Select **Cancel Subscription**

You’ll continue to have access to Disney+ until the end of your current subscription term – timing varies depending on your plan and if you were billed monthly, annually, or are within another promotional period.

If you need help canceling your subscription, please [contact us](https://help.disneyplus.com/contact-us).

**Third-party billed subscription**

If you signed up for [Disney+ through a third-party billing partner](https://help.disneyplus.com/csp?id=csp_article_content&article=third-party-subscription), the steps to cancel your subscription may vary. Please navigate to your billing partner’s help center or contact them to learn more.

**NOTE:** If you signed up for **Hulu**, **Hulu Premium**, or a **bundle plan** (billed directly by Hulu) you can continue to [manage your plan directly through Hulu](https://help.hulu.com/article/hulu-cancel-hulu-subscription) or [](https://help.hulu.com/contact-us)if eligible, you can manage your plan through Disney+.

## **How do I cancel my bundle plan with an existing subscription?** 

If you had an existing Hulu, ESPN, or HBO Max subscription before you upgraded to a bundle plan, there are some additional complexities to be aware of when canceling your bundle plan.

**When you cancel your original subscription**, your subscription to your Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Bundle will continue uninterrupted, and you’ll continue to have access to Disney+, Hulu, and ESPN through that bundle plan. If you had been receiving a credit towards the monthly price of your Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Bundle plan for ESPN, the credit will end when you cancel your original subscription.

**When you cancel your Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Bundle**, your original Hulu, ESPN, or third-party billed subscription will continue at the then-current price and terms (unless you _also_ cancel the Hulu, ESPN, or third-party billed subscription separately). Moving forward, you’ll continue to be billed for your original plan by the biller you signed up through.

**NOTE:** If you signed up for a **Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Bundle plan through Hulu**, you can continue to [manage your plan directly through Hulu](https://help.hulu.com/article/hulu-cancel-hulu-subscription) or you can manage your plan through Disney+. If you signed up for a **Disney+, Hulu, HBO Max Bundle plan through HBO Max** please [manage your plan directly through HBO Max](https://help.hbomax.com/US-en/answer/detail/000002526) or [contact HBO Max support](https://help.hbomax.com/US-en/Home/Index) for further guidance.

## **Can I pause my Disney+ subscription?**

Not planning to stream for a little while? If you have a **monthly subscription billed by Disney+\***, you can pause it by following these steps through a computer or mobile browser:

1.  Log in to your [Disney+ account](https://www.disneyplus.com/account) 
2.  Select your **Profile**
3.  Select **Account**
4.  Select your **Disney+ subscription** under Subscription
5.  Select **Pause Subscription**
6.  Choose the duration of the pause
7.  Select **Pause Subscription**

_\*Certain plans may not be eligible for a subscription pause._

The pause will take effect on the first day of your next billing period, and you can continue streaming until then. Once the pause begins, you will not be charged and you will not have access to any content until the pause ends. If you have a bundle subscription, please note that pausing Disney+ will also pause the other streaming services that are part of your bundle plan.

If you have an [Extra Member](https://help.disneyplus.com/article/disneyplus-extra-member) or an add-on with your subscription, that will be paused as well. While the Extra Member won’t receive a direct email notification, they will see a message when trying to stream content and can view the pause status in their account settings.

When the pause ends, you’ll be able to stream again and a new billing period will begin.

If you change your mind during the pause, you can resume your subscription at any time by selecting **Resume Subscription** on your [Disney+ account management page](https://disneyplus.com/account). When you select this option, you will be billed immediately, starting a new billing period.

Please keep in mind that in order to be eligible to pause, your Disney+ account should be active and your payments need to be up to date.

**Third-party billing**

If your Disney+ subscription is billed through a [third-party billing partner](https://help.disneyplus.com/csp?id=csp_article_content&article=third-party-subscription), please contact your billing partner directly for further assistance.

**NOTE:** If you signed up for **Hulu**, **Hulu Premium**, or a **bundle plan** (billed directly by Hulu) you can continue to [manage and pause your plan directly through Hulu](https://help.hulu.com/article/hulu-subscription-hold), or [](https://help.hulu.com/contact-us)if eligible, you can pause your plan through Disney+.

## [](https://help.disneyplus.com/article/disneyplus-cancel#)**How do I restart my Disney+ subscription after canceling?**

If you’ve recently made a cancellation request but changed your mind **before the end of your subscription term**, you’ll be able to restart your Disney+ subscription and continue without interruptions. To restart your subscription, follow these steps through a computer or mobile browser:

1.  Log in to [DisneyPlus.com](https://www.disneyplus.com/)
2.  Select your profile
3.  Select **Account**
4.  Select **Restart Subscription**
5.  Select **Agree** to confirm 

Please note that once your subscription term ends, your subscription will be fully canceled and not available for restart. If you want to continue streaming, you'll need to subscribe again.

**Third-party billing**

If you signed up for Disney+ through a [third-party billing partner](https://help.disneyplus.com/csp?id=csp_article_content&article=third-party-subscription), please contact your provider directly for further assistance on restarting.
