# What is your return policy?

**Extended Holiday Returns**

Holiday purchases made beginning November 2, 2026 will be eligible for return or exchange through January 24, 2027. Promotional gifts and free items received as part of a qualifying purchase must be returned if the qualifying merchandise is returned. 

**Our Return & Exchange Policy**

We want you to love your Pandora purchase! Here’s a simple guide to returning or exchanging your items.

* * *

**Return Eligibility**

You can return items that are unused and in original condition within 30 days of purchase with your original receipt or electronic receipt.

To be eligible for a refund, returned items, must include all original stones and components. Pandora may deny a return or refund if inspection determines that an item has been altered, modified, damaged, tampered with, or has had any stone or component removed, substituted, or replaced after purchase.

* * *

**Final Sale Items**

The following items are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged, unless they arrive damaged or defective:

*   Engraved items
    
*   Last Chance items
    
*   Gift Cards

* * *

**Damaged or Defective Items**

If your item arrives damaged or defective, you may return it by mail or in-store for assistance.

* * *

**Returns By Mail**

Returning your item is easy and free. Just follow the steps below:

1.  Visit [**Check Order Status**](https://us.pandora.net/en/join-mypandora) and enter your order number and email address to locate your order. You do **not** need to have or sign in to a Pandora account to start a return.
2.  Select **Register Return / View Label** next to the order you'd like to return.
3.  Choose the item(s) you wish to return, select the quantity and reason for return, then click **Confirm Return**.
4.  Once your return is registered, you'll receive:
    *   A **UPS return barcode** that can be scanned at a participating UPS Store if you don't have access to a printer.
    *   A **downloadable PDF shipping label** if you prefer to print and attach it to your package yourself.
5.  Securely pack your item(s).
6.  Bring your package to a **UPS Store**, **UPS Authorized Shipping Outlet**, or **UPS Customer Center**. If you're using the barcode option, simply show the barcode to a UPS associate and they'll print the shipping label for you.

**Return shipping is on us, so you won't be charged.**

\* If you experience any issues accessing your return barcode or shipping label, please contact our Pandora Jewelry Customer Care Center via chat for assistance.

* * *

**Refunds & Processing Times**

Once your package is received and validated by our warehouse, we’ll process your return and send you a confirmation email.

*   Refunds may take up to 7 business days after validation, but we aim to process them within 5 business days.
    
*   All refunds are issued to the original form of payment. Please note:
    
    *   Original shipping charges are not refundable.
        
    *   Banks may take an additional 3–5 business days to credit your account.
        
    *   Gift Cards are non-returnable and not refundable once purchased.

* * *

**Returns In-Store**

Prefer to return your item in person? Visit a **Pandora store** and our store associates will be happy to help.

*   Bring the item you'd like to return along with your packing slip or email invoice.
*   Refunds will be issued to your original form of payment.
*   Purchases made with PayPal will be refunded as a **Pandora Gift Card**.
*   Use our **Store Finder** to locate your nearest Pandora store: [**Store Finder**](https://stores.pandora.net/en-us/)

* * *

**Exchanges**

If you’d like a different item:

*   First, return the original item and receive a refund
    
*   Then, purchase your new item
    
*   In-store exchanges for items of equal or lesser value may be available, depending on store inventory
