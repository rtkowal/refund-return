# Peloton Membership: Identifying and Canceling a Peloton App Membership

_**Any changes to Peloton App Membership billing, including cancellation, must be actioned directly through the platform that was used to first purchase the App Membership originally.**_ 

* * *

If you originally purchased your App Membership **directly through Peloton**, your App Membership is considered "**first-party**". 

Detailed instructions for how to process a **first-party Peloton App Membership** cancellation can be found [here](https://support.onepeloton.com/s/article/Peloton-Membership-How-To-Manage-Your-App-Membership), in the section entitled "**Canceling Your Peloton App Membership**".

* * *

If you originally purchased your App Membership **via a third-party platform**, your App Membership is considered "**third-party**". Third-party platforms include: 

*   iOS
*   AppleTV
*   Android
*   AndroidTV
*   Amazon
*   Roku (not available in Germany and Australia)

Detailed instructions for how to process a **third-party Peloton App Membership** cancellation can be found [here](https://support.onepeloton.com/s/article/Peloton-Membership-How-To-Manage-Your-Third-Party-App-Membership), within the section that **corresponds to the specific third-party platform**.
