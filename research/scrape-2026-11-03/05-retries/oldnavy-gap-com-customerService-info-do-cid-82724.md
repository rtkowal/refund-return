# Customer Service - Returns and Exchanges

Return or Exchange

![gift icon](https://oldnavyprod.a.bigcontent.io/v1/static/Gifts)Returning or exchanging a gift? Please select the purchase method from above.

  

Return Policy Quick Facts

*   Free In-Store Return and Exchange
    *   Available for eligible\* purchases
  
*   Returns by Mail (online purchases only)
    *   Free for Encore Members
    *   A $6 return fee applies to returns by mail from nonmembers
    *   Items shipped & sold by third-party sellers are eligible for free returns.
    *   Items purchased on third-party sites or apps are subject to the return policies of those sites.
    *   Use a prepaid return shipping label or third-party drop-off QR code
  
*   30 Days to Return or Exchange
    *   From the delivery date for online purchases
    *   From the purchase date for store purchases
  
*   Some Encore Members have extended return windows:
    *   Premier Members have up to 45 days to return; All-Access Cardmembers have up to 60 days. Returns after 30 days will receive a Merchandise Return Card (MRC).

\*Full details can be found on our [Return Policy FAQ page](https://oldnavy.gap.com/customerService/info.do?cid=82724&cs=return_policy_faq) .

Items purchased through a 3rd Party Seller need to be returned through the 3rd Party seller. Select the Online Purchase Channel for more details.
