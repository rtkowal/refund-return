# Abercrombie.com

We hope you love your order – but just in case something isn't quite right, we've made it easy to make a return.

If you are not satisfied with your purchase, return it to us for a refund, subject to the following terms.

Select merchandise will be accompanied with a tag that must stay attached in order to return the item.

We’ve made it easy to make a return online – [click here](https://www.abercrombie.com/shop/wd/help/startmyreturn) to get started.

To receive your refund to your original payment method, your online return must be initiated within 60 days of the order shipment date. After 60 days of your order shipment date, the only option for refund will be offered in the form of an e-gift card, sent to the email address used on the order.

    If an invoice was not included in your package, and you submit your return online selecting “I’ll Ship it Myself”, please print your order confirmation email that was sent to you at order placement and include it in the return package.

To return your merchandise without initiating the return online, please send the merchandise back to us at the address below. You are responsible for return shipping costs. Returns sent back to us by your own return carrier must be received and processed at our Distribution Center within 60 days of the order shipment date to receive a refund to your original payment method.

    If an invoice was not included in your package, and you do not submit your return online, please print your order confirmation email that was sent to you at order placement and include it in the return package.

Abercrombie & Fitch Returns Department  
200 Abercrombie Way  
New Albany, OH 43054  
US

We refund any merchandise in resalable condition with a copy of your original invoice to the original payment method if mailed to the address listed above within 60 days of the shipping date. If your return is received and processed at our Distribution Center after 60 days of the order shipment date, your refund will be processed in the form of an e-gift card.

If your order was placed in USD, you may return in a US Abercrombie & Fitch or abercrombie kids store.
